What mobile pet grooming costs in Dallas–Fort Worth

In Dallas–Fort Worth, a mobile full groom runs $78–$105 for a small dog, $105–$145 for a medium dog, $132–$178 for a large dog, and $165–$222 for an XL dog (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming, 2026). A bath and brush costs less — $65–$88 small, $88–$118 medium, $112–$148 large. Doodles sit higher: $118–$188 for a full groom. The van in your driveway and one-on-one time are what you pay extra for over a salon.
