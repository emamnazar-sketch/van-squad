## Data-quality caveats

1. **Not individually re-verified.** Phones and websites come from Google Maps search-result snippets and company websites found via web search; each entry was not opened and re-checked against the business's own site. Treat phone numbers as "as published in the directory listing" until confirmed.
2. **Wallaaah Car Wash and Mobile Detailing** was flagged "possibly temporarily closed" in its Google listing — verify it's operating before any outreach.
3. **Missing phones:** "Not shown in public snippet" means the search result showed a website but no phone number (K9 Cosmetology, Groombuggy, Alpha Grooming, Art Pet Grooming, Pride Mobile, Ricky's, Tail Wagon, That Detail Guy). The phone is likely on their website or Yelp page — a quick manual check per business would fill these gaps.
4. **Mobile-only filter for groomers:** salon-based groomers (Bath & Biscuits, Bark Boutique, Shear Madness, Pet Stylista, Elk Grove salon groomers, etc.) were deliberately excluded since the directory targets mobile/on-site services. "Good Dog mobile grooming" (El Dorado Hills) appeared in results but had no public phone or website, so it was dropped.
5. **Fringe geography:** 4 Paws Only (Shingle Springs, ~25 mi east of Sacramento) markets itself to Sacramento (domain: groomingsacramento.com) but is outside the core metro — flag if outreach is strictly Sacramento-city.
6. **Barkbus and Groombuggy** are multi-city brands/franchises, not local indies — fine as directory listings, but expect a corporate decision-maker rather than an owner-operator.
7. **Chain car washes excluded:** Quick Quack, Orbit Wash and similar fixed-location car washes were excluded; only mobile/on-site detailers were kept.
8. **No personal contact info included.** All phone numbers and websites are business-published. No owner names, personal emails, or personal cell numbers were collected.
9. **Nobody has been contacted.** This is a research list only — no calls, texts, or emails sent.
