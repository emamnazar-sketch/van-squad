Service guide · Home cleaning

Home cleaning that fits your space

Standard upkeep or a deep reset — mobile cleaners come to you. Compare on scope, not just price.

What to compare

  • Home size and task list: bedrooms, baths and extras like inside the oven change the job — list them.
  • Supplies and pets: ask whether they bring supplies, and secure pets before the visit.
  • Standard vs deep clean: a first visit is often deeper (and priced higher) than recurring upkeep.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not necessarily — many customers hand over a key or code. Agree access and your priority rooms directly with the cleaner.

Typical pricing. A sample two-bedroom standard clean on Van Squads is $120, travel included.
