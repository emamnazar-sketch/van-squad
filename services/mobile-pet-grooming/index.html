Mobile pet grooming with your pet in mind

One-on-one grooming in a van outside your home — calmer for anxious pets, easier for you. What to check first:

What to compare

  • Pet size, temperament and health: groomers price by size and coat, and need to know about anxiety or medical issues.
  • Temperament policy: ask how they handle nervous pets and what happens if a groom cannot be finished safely.
  • Where the grooming happens: a fully equipped van, your bathroom, or the driveway — each has trade-offs.

Is mobile grooming good for nervous dogs?

Often yes — there is no noisy salon, no cages and no other animals. Tell the groomer about triggers when you request.

Typical pricing. Sample small-dog bath & tidy packages on Van Squads run about $105–$110, travel included.
