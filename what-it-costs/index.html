What mobile services cost

Nobody likes guessing what a job will run. Below are typical price ranges for the main services in this directory, pulled from our own published price guides. Your real price depends on the size of the job, your home or car, and where you live — so use these to plan, then get an exact quote from a local pro.

House cleaning

$118–$238 per regular visit

Most people pay in this range for a regular clean, with about $176 the middle. Cleaners who bill by the hour charge about $25–$50 per cleaner per hour. Bigger homes and first-time cleans sit at the high end.

Deep cleaning

$180–$375 for most homes

A one-time, top-to-bottom clean costs more than a regular visit because it covers the stuff regular cleans skip — inside the oven, baseboards, the works. Most homes land in this range, around $260 on average.

Handyman

$50–$150 per hour

Most handymen charge in this range, with $75–$90 an hour typical. Almost every visit has a minimum of about $125–$250 for the first hour or two, and a typical project totals around $405 all-in.

Mobile auto detailing

$99–$350 for most jobs

A basic wash and wax starts around $99. A full interior or exterior detail runs $249–$299, and a complete top-to-bottom detail is $349–$499 or more. Trucks and three-row SUVs cost more than sedans.

Mobile pet grooming

$60–$160 per full groom

Size drives this one: roughly $60–$90 for a small dog, $80–$130 for a medium dog, and $100–$160 or more for a large dog. That covers a bath, haircut, nails, and ears in a van at your curb.

Pool cleaning & maintenance

$140–$275 per month

A full weekly plan — skimming, brushing, chemicals, and equipment checks — usually lands in this range per month. One-time cleanups and green-pool rescues are priced per visit and cost more.

Pressure washing

$150–$270 for a driveway

A standard driveway runs in this range. Washing the whole house costs more — about $400–$700 for a 1,500 to 2,000 square foot home. Decks and patios are usually priced by size.

Read these ranges the right way

These are typical ranges, not quotes. Two homes on the same street can get different prices because one job is simply bigger than the other. When you ask for quotes, tell the pro the size of the job up front — square footage, number of bedrooms, car size, dog size — and the quotes you get back will be much closer to the final bill.

Common questions

Are these exact prices?

No. These are typical ranges from our published price guides. Your price depends on the size of the job, where you live, and who you hire. The only exact price is a quote from a local pro.

Why does the same job cost more in one city than another?

Labor costs, drive time, and how busy local pros are all move the price. Big cities usually sit at the high end of a range, smaller towns at the low end.
