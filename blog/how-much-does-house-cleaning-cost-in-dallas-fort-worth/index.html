How much does house cleaning cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

The short answer. In Dallas, the average family spends $163 a visit on house cleaning (Angi), and Thumbtack puts hiring a pro in Dallas at $181–$322. By the hour, Angi puts Dallas at about $50 an hour, ranging $25–$75, while Care.com’s listed cleaners average $21.61 an hour in Dallas and $19.89 an hour in Fort Worth. A standard clean from a Dallas company runs $140–$280, a deep clean $220–$440, and a move-out clean $350–$600 or more (Dallas Maids).

Those hourly numbers are not measuring the same hire. Care.com’s $21.61 is a listed individual cleaner. Angi’s $50 is the company rate, and a company often sends two people — about $100 an hour for the whole visit.

This is where people overpay. Compare the finished price for your home, not the hourly number.

What changes the price in Dallas–Fort Worth?

Size, first. Angi prices Dallas by the square foot: a standard clean runs $0.10–$0.20 per square foot, so a 1,500 square foot home runs $150–$300 and the median 2,200 square foot home runs $220–$440. Each extra bathroom adds $10–$40.

Dust and tile. Angi calls out Dallas dust and allergens, and notes tile floors are common in Dallas kitchens, bathrooms and laundry rooms because they suit the hot climate — grout takes time. Older homes in Lakewood (75214), White Rock (75218) and Lake Highlands (75243) carry more of that work than a new build in Plano.

Humidity on the Fort Worth side. Thumbtack’s Fort Worth guidance flags the humid climate: mold in bathrooms and warped wood floors in older homes need slower work, and that can move a Fort Worth quote up even when the hourly listings look lower.

How often they come. Angi puts a biweekly Dallas cleaning at $95–$240 against $120–$290 for a monthly one.

Standard, deep, or move-out — which one are you actually buying?

A standard clean covers the regular rooms: kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, floors, dusting. A deep clean adds the buildup — about $0.25 per square foot on Angi’s Dallas page, $220–$440 on Dallas Maids’ list.

Move-out is the priciest: about $0.35 per square foot (Angi), $770 at 2,200 square feet, $350–$600 or more (Dallas Maids). If the home is not empty, you are paying for work you do not need.

Add-ons are priced apart. On Angi’s Dallas list: fridge $10–$40, oven $20–$40, windows $4–$10 per window, laundry $5–$20 a load.

How to pay less and still get a clean house

  • Go recurring. Weekly or biweekly visits cost less per visit because the home never gets far behind.
  • Book the deep clean once, then maintain. Pay the deep price first, then drop to the standard rate.
  • Compare finished prices, not hourly rates. One cleaner for four hours and a two-person company done in two can cost about the same.
  • Name the add-ons in writing. Fridge, oven, inside windows. If it is not named, it is not included.
