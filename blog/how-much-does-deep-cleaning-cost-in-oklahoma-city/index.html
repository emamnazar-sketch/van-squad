How much does deep cleaning cost in Oklahoma City?

The short answer. In Oklahoma City, a deep cleaning runs $200–$400 for a typical home (CottageCare, Downtown Oklahoma City). On one local price list, a one-time deep clean is twice the weekly rate — $150 for under 1,000 sq ft, $190 for 1,001–2,000 sq ft, and $240 for 2,001–3,000 sq ft (Clean Pros). Company crews bill $40–$75 an hour locally; an independent maid averages about $15 an hour (Care.com).

This is where people overpay. You are not comparing the same job if one quote is hourly and the other is flat. A $15-an-hour independent and a $60-an-hour crew can land in the same place — the crew just finishes faster. Ask what is included, and whether inside the oven, inside the fridge, and the window tracks are in or extra.

A deep clean adds the work a regular visit skips: baseboards, grout, ceiling fans, blinds and window tracks, and the grime behind and under things. That is why the local list prices it at double a weekly visit.

What changes the price in Oklahoma City?

Home size and bathrooms. Clean Pros prices Oklahoma City homes by square footage. A 2,001–3,000 sq ft home in Edmond runs $240 on that list, against $150 for a home under 1,000 sq ft. Bathrooms move the number most after size — each one is scrubbing and grout, not just floor space.

Red dirt and storm dust. Spring storms push Oklahoma red dirt and fine dust into entryways, window tracks, and along baseboards. A home in Moore or Norman that went through a dusty spring with the windows open is a longer job than the same house kept closed up. Condition is what a walkthrough is pricing.

How long since the last deep clean. A first clean is usually a deep clean, even if nobody calls it that. Built-up grease and grout take time the first time through. Once the home is reset, a weekly clean on the same list drops back to $75–$120 for most sizes — the deep price is a one-time reset, not your ongoing rate.

How to pay less without getting a thin clean

  • Clear the clutter before they arrive. Every minute spent moving toys and mail is a minute not spent on grout. Thumbtack makes the same point nationally: declutter first and the crew works faster.
  • Point the deep clean at the rooms that need it. Kitchens and bathrooms carry most of the buildup. If money is tight, deep clean those two and keep bedrooms on a standard clean.
  • Bundle the add-ons into this visit. Inside the oven and fridge cost less added to a visit already in the house than booked as a separate trip later.
  • Follow it with a regular schedule. A deep clean twice a year plus biweekly visits usually costs less than one big rescue clean after everything has built back up.

What is included in a deep cleaning that a standard clean skips?

Size and condition, mostly. A home over 3,000 sq ft, heavy buildup, or add-ons push past the typical range. Nationally, Angi puts a deep clean at $180–$375, average $260 — get the included list in writing and compare that, not just the total.
