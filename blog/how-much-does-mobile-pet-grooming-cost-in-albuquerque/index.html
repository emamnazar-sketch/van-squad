How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Albuquerque?

The short answer. Mobile grooming in Albuquerque runs $65–$80 for a small dog, $75–$100 for a medium dog, and $90–$130 for a large dog (HeiBob’s 2026 Albuquerque price table). A salon full groom costs less — $45–$60 small, $55–$80 medium, $75–$110 large — and mobile adds a $15–$30 premium on top. What you get for that premium is the van in your driveway and a groomer working on just your dog.

What changes the price in Albuquerque?

Size and coat, first. The table above is really a time table. A large double-coated dog that hikes the bosque and comes home full of burrs and dust takes longer to wash, dry, and brush out than a short-coated small dog. De-shedding alone adds $20–$55 depending on size.

The dry, dusty climate. Albuquerque dogs pick up dust and burrs outdoors, and most do best on a 4–8 week schedule (HeiBob). A dog kept on that schedule is a routine visit. A dog left three months, matted underneath, is a longer and pricier one.

Where you sit on the route. Van Squads’ Albuquerque mobile groomers cluster on the Westside — four of the five listed businesses cover 87120 — with coverage reaching downtown (87102), Old Town and the North Valley (87104, 87107), Nob Hill (87106), East Central (87108), and the far north side (87113, 87114). A home on a groomer’s regular route is an easy slot. A one-off trip across town is harder to book.

One dog, start to finish. A mobile groomer works on your dog alone — no cage wait, no other dogs in the van. For an anxious or senior dog that is often the whole point. It is also why mobile costs more: the salon batches dogs, the van does not.

How to pay less without a worse groom

  • Stay on a 4–6 week schedule. Shorter, routine visits cost less than rescue grooms, and regulars get the route slots first.
  • Brush between visits. A few minutes at home keeps mats from forming, and mats are paid time at the van.
  • Ask about the second dog. The van is already at your curb — a second dog in the same stop usually costs less than a separate visit.
  • Compare what is included. One quote may bundle nails, ears, and glands; another adds them at $10–$22 each. Line them up before you call one cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Albuquerque?

$65–$80 for a small dog, $75–$100 for a medium dog, and $90–$130 for a large dog (HeiBob’s 2026 Albuquerque table). Local mobile full grooms run $65–$120 depending on the operator and your dog’s breed and coat.

Why does mobile grooming cost more than a salon in Albuquerque?

Mobile adds a $15–$30 premium over salon prices here. You are paying for the van, the drive, and one-on-one time with just your dog — no drop-off, no cage, no wait.

How often should I groom my dog in Albuquerque’s climate?

Every 4–8 weeks for most dogs (HeiBob). Longer coats, or dogs that spend time on the bosque or in the mountains, may need it more often because of burrs and debris. Short-coated breeds can go 8–12 weeks.

Do Albuquerque mobile groomers require vaccinations?

Most Albuquerque salons require proof of current rabies, distemper (DHPP), and Bordetella vaccinations. Mobile groomers are typically more flexible, since your dog is not around other animals during the visit (HeiBob). Ask when you book so there is no surprise at the van.
