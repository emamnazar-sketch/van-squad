How much does pressure washing cost in Fargo?

The short answer. In Fargo, pressure washing runs $70 to $1,700 depending on the task on HomeBlue’s Fargo page. A house wash averages about $500 — $400 to $700 for a 1,500 to 2,000 square foot house. A standard 40 x 20 foot driveway averages about $220 ($160 to $280), and a 200 square foot deck or patio averages about $90 ($70 to $110). Thumbtack’s Fargo page puts the rate at $0.10 to $0.17 per square foot of property size.

This is where people overpay. A deck at $70 to $110 on its own is a small trip for a crew to make alone. Add that same deck to a house wash or a driveway visit and it just shares the trip out — one setup, one pack-up. Price the visit, not just the surface.

What changes the price in Fargo?

Size is the whole quote, surface by surface. HomeBlue’s Fargo page doesn’t give one house price — it steps by size, from $400 to $700 at 1,500 to 2,000 square feet up to $1,000 to $1,700 at 4,000 to 5,000. Driveways step the same way, from $160 to $280 for 800 square feet to $390 to $690 for 2,000. Measure what you actually want washed before you call. A quote built on a guess gets corrected on your driveway, usually upward.

November is the end of the season here, not the middle of it. HomeGuide puts the pressure washing season between March and November, before freezing temperatures arrive. Thumbtack’s Fargo page says the best time is a warm, sunny day, after the rainy, storm season has passed, so the house can dry out. In Fargo in mid-November you are booking the last good days, not a routine spring slot. If a job needs heated water — power washing, in HomeBlue’s terms — expect 10 to 15% more than the cold-water price on that same Fargo page.

Coverage in the directory sits in Fargo proper, with one reach across the river. Three of the four Fargo pressure washing listings on Van Squads cover ZIPs 58102, 58103, and 58104 — that includes south Fargo, Rose Creek and Osgood included in 58104. The fourth covers 58104 plus Horace (58047) and Moorhead (56560), on the Minnesota side. If you’re in West Fargo (58078), say so up front — it’s not in any of these four listings’ ZIPs, and travel is part of the price.

The Fargo guides don’t agree, and that’s useful, not a problem. Manta’s Fargo page says homeowners in Cass County paid $147 to $592 on average for exterior pressure cleaning in 2025. HomeBlue’s Fargo house range starts at $400 for a smaller house. Those are different jobs counted different ways — a county average across exterior cleaning against a house-only range by size. Get two written quotes for the same surfaces, and ask each crew what is in and what is out.

How to pay less and still get the work done

  • Bundle the surfaces into one visit. House, driveway, and the small deck in one trip. One setup beats three minimum trips.
  • Measure before you call. Home size, driveway length and width, deck size. The Fargo price steps are by size, so your numbers set the quote.
  • Ask cold or hot, in writing. Cold-water pressure washing and heated power washing are not the same job or the same price — HomeBlue puts the heated process 10 to 15% higher in Fargo.
  • Clear the surface yourself. HomeBlue’s Fargo page lists moving furniture off the surface among the things that push a price up. Move it before the crew arrives.
