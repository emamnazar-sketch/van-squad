How much does house cleaning cost in Williston?

The short answer. A standard house cleaning in Williston runs $118 to $255 on MaidProfit's North Dakota page, which puts Williston cleaners at $25 to $44 an hour per cleaner — the highest rates in the state, a gap MaidProfit ties to the Bakken oil boom. Hiring an individual instead of a company costs less: Care.com's Williston page puts the starting rate at $19.33 an hour, and its Williston housekeepers average $18.00 to $18.25 an hour posted.

The spread confuses people, so read it by who shows up. An individual housekeeper posting on Care.com charges for her own time — $18 to $19 an hour at the Williston average. A company sends insured cleaners, brings supplies, and covers a no-show with a backup. That is the $25 to $44 band, and CleanSpace puts Williston independents who run as a business higher still, at $35 to $60 an hour.

This is where people overpay in Williston. They compare a company's flat quote against an individual's hourly post and assume the company is padding. Run the hours. A two-cleaner team at $35 an hour each for two hours is $140 of labor before supplies and drive time — right inside the standard range.

Why does Williston top the state?

Labor is tight and time is short. MaidProfit says it plainly: the Bakken oil boom pushed up the cost of living and wages around Williston, and cleaning prices followed. CleanSpace's Williston guide adds the schedule side — a lot of local workers run two weeks on, two weeks off, or 80-hour weeks in the field. On your off week you do not want to spend a day scrubbing. You pay for the hours back, and you pay Williston rates for them.

Ask about shift-friendly scheduling when you book. A cleaner who can come on a weekday while a night-shift worker sleeps in another part of the house, or who can hit the house the day a rotation starts, is worth more here than a slightly lower rate with a fixed 9-to-5 window.

What does the dirt look like in Williams County?

It changes by season, and both seasons add time. CleanSpace's Williston guide names the pair: winter brings snow, slush, and road salt tracked onto floors and into mudrooms, and summer brings dry wind that blows fine prairie dust and Bakken clay into the house. Clay-rich mud off a work truck is not regular dust. It needs soaking and a second pass at the entry, and that is cleaner time you will see in the quote.

If your place sits out toward Trenton or Watford City on an acreage, say so up front. CleanSpace notes rural homes take the worst of the mud and that cleaners charge more for the travel and the heavier work. A quote built on an in-town assumption will not hold.

How much do deep cleans and move-outs cost?

MaidProfit publishes no Williston row for these, so use its North Dakota figures and expect the top half locally: deep cleaning $220 to $430 statewide, move-out cleaning $250 to $470 statewide. Williston's standard cleaning already tops the state table, and turnover is steady here — CleanSpace points to rentals, corporate housing, and homes in Harvest Hills and Pheasant Run changing hands often, which keeps move-out work in demand.

For a move-out, get the landlord's checklist before you book, not after. Deposit money turns on details like inside the oven and the window tracks. Hand the cleaner that list and ask for the flat price against it.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a standard house cleaning in Williston?

$118 to $255 on MaidProfit's Williston row. Its North Dakota standard average is $158, and Williston sits at the top of the state table, so a mid-size Williston home usually lands in the upper half of that range.

Why is Williston the most expensive city in North Dakota for cleaning?

MaidProfit ties it to the Bakken oil boom: higher living costs and higher wages around Williston push cleaning prices above the state average. Tight labor does the rest — there are not many cleaners to go around, and shift workers pay to get their off time back.

Should I hire an individual or a company in Williston?

An individual costs less per hour — Care.com's Williston housekeepers post $10 to $26 an hour, averaging about $18. A company at $25 to $44 an hour per cleaner brings insurance, supplies, and a backup if your cleaner gets sick. Pick on whether a missed visit breaks your week.

How much does a move-out clean cost in Williston?

Use the North Dakota range of $250 to $470 from MaidProfit as your planning figure, and expect the top half in Williston. Turnover in rentals and corporate housing keeps demand steady, and a landlord checklist adds tasks a standard clean skips.

Does winter change the price in Williston?

It changes the work more than the rate card. Snow, slush, and road salt mean more entry and floor time, and spring thaw brings clay mud. A cleaner who quotes the same minutes in January as in June has not cleaned here long.
