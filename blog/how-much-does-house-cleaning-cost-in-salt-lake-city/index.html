How much does house cleaning cost in Salt Lake City?

The short answer. In Salt Lake City, a standard house cleaning runs $135 to $275 on MaidProfit's city row, at $28 to $46 an hour per cleaner. Care.com puts an individual Salt Lake cleaner at about $21.28 an hour — solo cleaners, not companies. Local companies price by the visit: Queen of Maids starts recurring plans at $149 every two weeks, and Wasatch Cleaners starts a one-time standard clean at $219 for a small home, $289 for a 3-bedroom.

This is where people overpay. A $149 biweekly Partial Clean and a $319 deep clean aren't the same job at two prices. The Partial covers the kitchen, bathrooms, floors, and trash in a home that already stays tidy. The deep clean adds the built-up work — behind appliances, baseboards, grout. If your home gets cleaned every two weeks, you need the $149 kind of price, not the $319 one.

What changes the price in Salt Lake City?

Inversion season puts dust inside your home. From December to February, warm air traps cold air in the Salt Lake Valley and the haze sits for days. Wolfnest's Utah rental guide notes that poor outdoor air in inversion season means more dust and particles settling indoors — on carpets, upholstery, ceiling fans, and vents. A home in West Valley City (84119) that keeps its windows shut for weeks still collects that film. Dusting that actually covers fans and vents is worth asking about before a winter booking.

Utah homes run big, and big homes take longer. MaidProfit makes the point on its Utah page: the state has some of the largest average family sizes in the country, so homes are bigger and take more cleaning time. Wasatch Cleaners' ladder shows it in dollars — $219 for a 1–2 bedroom place, $289 for a 3-bedroom family home, $345 for a 4–5 bedroom home, all in normal condition. Count bedrooms and bathrooms before you compare two quotes.

How often you book is the discount. Wasatch Cleaners prices a one-time standard clean from $219, but recurring visits start lower: $186.15 weekly, $197.10 biweekly, $208.05 monthly. Queen of Maids builds its whole Salt Lake pricing around biweekly plans, from $149 to $225 depending on how much of the home is covered. MaidProfit's Utah ranges agree: weekly $100 to $215 a visit against $130 to $265 monthly.

Deep and move-out work sits in its own tier. Wasatch Cleaners starts deep cleaning at $319 and move-in/move-out at $329, against $219 for a standard visit. MaidProfit's Utah ranges put deep cleans at $235 to $475 and move-outs at $260 to $510. Don't budget a move-out in Holladay (84124) from the standard-clean price — an empty home gets checked room by room, and the quote reflects that.

How to pay less without a worse clean

  • Go recurring, even monthly. Every Salt Lake list checked prices repeat visits under one-time work for the same home.
  • Match the tier to the mess. A tidy home on a biweekly plan doesn't need the Premium tier with ceiling fans and vents every visit — save that for a seasonal deep clean.
  • Say the home's real size up front. Bedrooms and bathrooms set the flat rate at both Salt Lake operators above. A surprise extra bathroom reprices the visit.
  • Ask what inversion dust work is included. Fans, vents, and baseboards are deep-clean items on most lists. Naming them before you book is cheaper than adding them at the door.
