## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Buy the half that's dirty.** Interior-only starts at $149–$200, exterior-only at $69–$120. A full detail is the wrong buy when one side is fine.
- **Name the pet hair and the car seat up front.** Pet hair runs $35–$40 as an add-on, a child seat $45–$50. Surprises are how a $230 detail becomes a $300 one.
- **Wash the salt off early.** Getting brine off in the season is cleaning work. Leave it on till spring and you're paying for correction instead.
- **Ask about water and power before you book.** Tell the detailer what your driveway or lot has.
