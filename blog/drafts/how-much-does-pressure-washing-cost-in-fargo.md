## What changes the price in Fargo?

**Size is the whole quote, surface by surface.** HomeBlue's Fargo page doesn't give one house price — it steps by size, from $400 to $700 at 1,500 to 2,000 square feet up to $1,000 to $1,700 at 4,000 to 5,000. Driveways step the same way, from $160 to $280 for 800 square feet to $390 to $690 for 2,000. Measure what you actually want washed before you call. A quote built on a guess gets corrected on your driveway, usually upward.

**November is the end of the season here, not the middle of it.** HomeGuide puts the pressure washing season between March and November, before freezing temperatures arrive. Thumbtack's Fargo page says the best time is a warm, sunny day, after the rainy, storm season has passed, so the house can dry out. In Fargo in mid-November you are booking the last good days, not a routine spring slot. If a job needs heated water — power washing, in HomeBlue's terms — expect 10 to 15% more than the cold-water price on that same Fargo page.

## How to pay less and still get the work done

- **Bundle the surfaces into one visit.** House, driveway, and the small deck in one trip. One setup beats three minimum trips.
- **Measure before you call.** Home size, driveway length and width, deck size. The Fargo price steps are by size, so your numbers set the quote.
- **Ask cold or hot, in writing.** Cold-water pressure washing and heated power washing are not the same job or the same price — HomeBlue puts the heated process 10 to 15% higher in Fargo.
- **Clear the surface yourself.** HomeBlue's Fargo page lists moving furniture off the surface among the things that push a price up. Move it before the crew arrives.
