## What changes the price in Dallas–Fort Worth?

**Pollen, humidity and shade.** Spring pollen and humid summers leave mildew on shaded north walls in older Dallas neighborhoods like Lakewood, Preston Hollow and Highland Park. That green film is why most homes here get washed, and a wall that needs treatment costs more than a quick rinse of clean brick.

**Your house material.** Older homes in Highland Park and Lakewood often have softer mortar and older paint, so the crew has to go gentler and slower. Newer builds in Frisco, McKinney and Prosper can usually take a firmer wash.

**Size and stories.** Angi prices a 1,500 square foot Dallas house at $150–$750, a 2,000 square foot house at $200–$1,000 and a 2,500 square foot house at $250–$1,250. A second story and extra surfaces add time.

**One surface or the whole lot.** A single small job still carries the trip and setup. Adding the driveway and patio while the crew is there costs less per square foot than booking them separately later.

## Pressure washing, power washing, or soft washing?

They are not the same job, and the price tells you which one you were quoted.

HomeGuide puts plain pressure washing — cold water, high pressure — at $0.08–$0.20 per square foot nationally, and power washing, which adds hot water, at $0.35–$0.77 nationally. Hot water helps on greasy concrete. It is not needed on most siding.

Siding, stucco and roofs usually need a soft wash: low pressure with cleaning solution. If a quote for your siding is priced like a driveway blast, ask before anyone starts.

## How to pay less and still get a clean house

- **Bundle the flat work.** Driveway, walkway and patio in one visit. In Irving that driveway alone runs around $180 — bundling spreads the trip cost.
- **Name every surface in writing.** House, driveway, patio, fence. If it is not named, it is not included.
- **Ask what is being measured.** Thumbtack's Dallas guidance is $0.10–$0.17 per square foot of property size. A quote priced by the surface itself will read differently.
- **Wash before you paint or list.** Paint bonds to a clean surface. Do not pay for a full wash twice because the painter was booked first.
