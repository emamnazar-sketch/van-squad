# How much do you pay for regular house cleaning?

## The short answer

Most homeowners pay **$118–$238 per visit** for regular house cleaning in 2026, with a national average of **$176**. On a recurring schedule, weekly visits run **$75–$200** and biweekly visits **$100–$250** — regular bookings cost less per visit than one-time cleans because the home never gets far behind. Home size and bathroom count move the price most.

## How much does regular cleaning cost by schedule?

Frequency is the first price lever. The more often a cleaner comes, the less work each visit takes — and the lower the per-visit price.

| Schedule | Per visit (2026) | Per month (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | $75–$200 | $300–$800 |
| Biweekly | $100–$250 | $200–$500 |

For a 3-bedroom home, TIDY's 2026 guide puts the pattern the same way: weekly $100–$175 per visit, biweekly $120–$210, and monthly $150–$280 — with recurring plans typically discounted **10–20%** against one-time prices. Biweekly is the most popular schedule: it keeps a home consistently clean without paying for weekly visits.

## How much does it cost by home size?

Size is the second lever. Cleaners price by square footage, bedrooms, or both — a standard clean runs **$0.10–$0.20 per square foot**.

| Home | Regular cleaning per visit (2026) |
|---|---|
| 1-bedroom | $75–$110 |
| 2-bedroom | $100–$150 |
| 3-bedroom | $130–$200 |
| 4-bedroom | $150–$210 |
| 1,000 sq ft | $100–$200 |
| 2,000 sq ft | $200–$400 |
| 3,000 sq ft | $300–$600 |

Each extra bedroom adds about **$25–$35** to a regular clean, and each extra bathroom adds **$10–$40** — bathrooms cost more than their size suggests because of tile, grout, fixtures, and mirrors. Hourly billing, where offered, runs **$25–$50 per cleaner per hour**.

## What is included in a regular clean?

A regular (standard) clean covers the rooms you live in: dusting surfaces, vacuuming and mopping floors, wiping kitchen counters and appliance exteriors, scrubbing bathrooms, and taking out the trash. Angi prices that routine-areas service at **$75–$175 per visit**.

What it does not include is the detail work — inside the oven or fridge, baseboards, window tracks, and grout scrubbing. That is deep-clean territory at **$200–$400 per visit**, and most pros will ask you to start there: if a home has not been professionally cleaned in over three months, TIDY notes the standard advice is one deep clean first, then cheaper regular visits to maintain it.

## What pushes the price up or down?

- **Who you hire.** Independent cleaners usually charge less per hour; companies charge more and build in insurance, supplies, background checks, and a replacement if your cleaner is sick.
- **Location.** High-cost cities run well above the national range; smaller markets run below it. The same clean can shift 50–80% between markets.
- **Condition and household.** Pets, kids, and a long gap since the last clean all add time — pet-hair work alone can add $10–$30 per visit.
- **Add-ons.** Price them before you book: window cleaning $4–$10 per window, carpet cleaning $75–$200, fridge cleaning $10–$40, oven cleaning $20–$40, laundry $5–$20 per load.
- **First-visit and extras fees.** Watch for trip fees ($10–$25 outside a core area), a supplies charge ($10–$20), 2–3 hour minimums on small homes, and cancellation fees of 50–100% inside 24–48 hours.

## How do you keep the regular price down?

Book recurring — weekly and biweekly plans save 10–20% per visit against one-time bookings, and FieldCamp puts the typical biweekly discount at 10–15%. Declutter before the visit so paid time goes to cleaning, not picking up. Skip add-ons you can do yourself, and compare at least two or three quotes: for the same home, quotes vary more than most people expect.

## FAQ

**How much do you pay for regular house cleaning?**
$118–$238 per visit for most homeowners in 2026, with a national average of $176. Weekly regular visits run $75–$200 and biweekly visits $100–$250.

**Is biweekly cleaning cheaper than monthly?**
Per visit, yes — biweekly visits cost less than monthly ones because less grime builds up between visits, and recurring schedules typically carry a 10–20% discount. Per month, biweekly costs more in total because you are buying twice as many visits.

**Do I need a deep clean before starting regular service?**
Usually, if your home has not been professionally cleaned in over three months. The first deep clean ($200–$400) resets the home; regular visits after that are faster and cheaper to maintain.

**How much does a cleaner charge per hour?**
$25–$50 per cleaner per hour in 2026, according to Angi. Many regular services quote a flat per-visit price instead, based on home size and schedule.

**What is not included in a regular clean?**
Inside-oven and inside-fridge cleaning, baseboards, window tracks, grout scrubbing, and laundry are normally add-ons or deep-clean work, priced separately — confirm the list before your first visit.
