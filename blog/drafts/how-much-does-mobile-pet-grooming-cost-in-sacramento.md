# How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Sacramento?

## The short answer

In Sacramento, you'll usually pay about **$110 to $160 for a bundled mobile full groom** — bath, haircut, nails, ears, and glands — and the exact price depends on your dog's size (Thumbtack). Some groomers charge by time instead, and that runs higher. One Sacramento-area mobile groomer charges **$145 for the first hour** for a dog under 50 lbs. A salon groom costs less — full service starts around **$40 for a small dog and $75 for a large dog** (HomeGuide) — because you're not paying for a van to come to your house.

## What changes the price in Sacramento?

**Size and coat.** Your dog sets the price, not the appointment slot. A large dog with a heavy coat just takes longer. Mats take extra time to work out, too. Some Sacramento-area mobile groomers only take dogs under 70 lbs for mobile visits, and slots for larger dogs are limited — so book those early.

**The route, not just the groom.** A mobile groomer drives a full salon van from house to house. If you're in Downtown or Midtown (95814, 95816), Land Park (95818), or Elk Grove (95758), you're on most local routes. A one-off trip to the edge of a groomer's area is harder to fit in, and it can cost more than a stop on a regular route day in your neighborhood.

**How long since the last groom.** A dog groomed every 4–6 weeks is a shorter visit, and shorter means cheaper. Let a coat go for months — it happens a lot after a Sacramento summer of backyard dust and shedding — and you'll pay for the de-matting and the extra bath time. The quote will show it.

## How to pay less without a worse groom

- **Get on a recurring route.** A standing 4–6 week slot keeps your dog's coat easy to work with, keeps each visit shorter, and puts you on the groomer's regular day in your part of Sacramento.
- **Brush between visits.** A few minutes with a brush at home is the cheapest way to skip a matting charge at the van.
- **Groom the second dog in the same visit.** HomeGuide notes prices can be significantly lower for a second and third dog groomed in the same stop — the van is already at your curb.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a mobile dog groomer cost in Sacramento?**
About $110–$160 for a bundled full groom, priced by your dog's size (Thumbtack). Some groomers charge by the hour instead — $145 for the first hour for a dog under 50 lbs at one Sacramento-area mobile groomer. Nationally, dog grooming averages $60–$80 across standard and mobile services (Thumbtack).

**Why does mobile grooming cost more than a salon in Sacramento?**
Because the groomer brings the salon to you — van, water, power, and one-on-one time with just your dog — and drives between homes instead of grooming a lot of dogs in one place. Salon full service starts around $40 for a small dog; mobile bundles run $110–$160.

**What is included in a full mobile groom?**
Bath, blow dry, haircut or scissor finish, nail trim, ear cleaning, and gland expression — that's the standard bundle (Thumbtack, HomeGuide). Add-ons like teeth brushing, flea treatment, and de-shedding cost extra, usually $10–$25 each.

**Can a mobile groomer take my large dog in Sacramento?**
Often, yes, but ask first. Some Sacramento-area mobile groomers limit mobile visits to dogs under 70 lbs and book larger dogs only in limited slots, because the van's tub and table have real size limits.
