# How much does house cleaning cost in Portland?

## The short answer

A standard house cleaning in Portland runs **$150–$300 a visit** from a company (MaidProfit, Portland row), and Thumbtack puts professional jobs at **$201–$358**. Companies charge **$32–$52 an hour per cleaner**. Hiring a person on your own costs less — Portland cleaners post an average of **$24.09 an hour** on Care.com — but that's one person, not a crew. Oregon has no sales tax, so the quote is the price you pay.

Portland is the priciest cleaning market in Oregon. MaidProfit says Portland and Bend carry the state's highest rates, and NW Maids puts Portland about 8% above the national average. A $280 quote is the top of the local range, not a markup.

This is where people overpay: the first visit. A first clean on a home that hasn't been touched in months gets priced like a deep clean. Oregon deep cleans run $255–$510 statewide. If a company quotes deep-clean money and calls it a standard first visit, ask what the second visit costs. That number tells you the real rate.

## What changes the price in Portland?

**Rain, mostly.** Months of wet weather mean mud and wet leaves tracked in at the door, and bathrooms that stay damp. MaidProfit notes the rainy climate means Oregon homes often need extra attention in mold-prone areas. A bathroom in Southwest Portland (97219) that never quite dries out is a longer scrub than the same room in August.

**Labor costs.** Oregon's minimum wage is $14.70 an hour, and the Portland metro rate is $15.95 (MaidProfit). That floor shows up in the $32–$52 an hour companies charge per cleaner. Portland quotes sit above Salem or Medford quotes for the same rooms, and the wage gap is a big part of why.

**Frequency.** Recurring visits cost less. Statewide, weekly runs $115–$235, biweekly $125–$255 and monthly $145–$285 (MaidProfit). NW Maids puts Portland recurring standard cleans at $150–$280. The cleaner knows your home, and the buildup never gets ahead of them.

**Size and bathrooms.** Bathrooms carry the scrubbing time, so a 3-bed, 3-bath in Beaverton (97005) prices above a 3-bed, 2-bath at the same square footage. Pets add time too — hair is real work.

## Company crew or a person you hire yourself?

At $24.09 an hour on Care.com, a listed individual costs well under a company's $32–$52 per cleaner. HomeGuide's Portland page puts it plainly: a private cleaner charges 20% to 50% less than a professional service. The trade-off is plain. If they're sick, nobody comes. A company sends a replacement, brings supplies, and carries the insurance. Plenty of households hire the individual and accept the gaps. Just price the gaps honestly.

## How to pay less and still get a good clean

- **Go recurring after the first visit.** Weekly and biweekly rates run under one-time prices. Pay the higher first-visit price once, then let the cheaper schedule hold it.
- **Name the extras before the visit.** Inside the oven, inside the fridge, interior windows — say it up front. Add-ons named mid-clean get priced mid-clean.
- **Use the no-tax break.** Oregon has no sales tax on cleaning, so compare quotes straight up. A Portland quote and a Vancouver, Washington quote aren't built the same way.
