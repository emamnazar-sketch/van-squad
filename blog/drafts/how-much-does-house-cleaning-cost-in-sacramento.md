# How much does house cleaning cost in Sacramento?

## The short answer

In Sacramento, a house cleaner costs about **$22.81 an hour** on average (Care.com, October 2026). A standard one-time cleaning for a typical home runs **$90 to $180**, and a deep cleaning runs **$230 to $600**. Recurring weekly or biweekly visits usually cost less per visit than one-time cleanings.

## What Sacramento homeowners actually pay

| Cleaning type | Typical Sacramento price |
|---|---|
| Cleaner by the hour | $22.78–$28.07 per hour |
| Apartment cleaning | $80–$100 |
| Standard clean, typical family home | $90–$180 (about $120 is common) |
| Deep cleaning | $230–$600 |
| Move-in / move-out cleaning | Priced like a deep clean — ask for a flat quote |

Sacramento runs a little cheaper than California overall: the statewide starting average is $24.51 an hour against Sacramento's $22.78.

## What changes the price in Sacramento?

**Home size and bathrooms.** Bedrooms set the baseline; bathrooms move the quote most. A 4-bedroom in Elk Grove or Roseville simply takes longer than a 2-bedroom condo in Midtown.

**First clean vs. recurring.** Almost every Sacramento cleaner charges more for the first visit — it is effectively a deep clean that brings the home to a maintainable level. After that, weekly and biweekly visits drop to the standard rate.

**The type of clean.** A standard visit covers kitchens, bathrooms, floors, and dusting. Deep cleaning adds the detail work: baseboards, inside appliances, grout, and windows. Move-in and move-out cleans are priced like deep cleans because the home is empty and every surface is in scope.

**Where you live in the metro.** Cleaners price in travel. Homes in Natomas, Land Park, and East Sacramento sit inside most service areas; outlying homes in Folsom or Lincoln may see a travel fee or a higher minimum.

## How to pay less without getting a worse clean

- **Book recurring.** A biweekly schedule is the sweet spot for most Sacramento homes — frequent enough that no visit starts from scratch, and priced lower per visit than one-time cleans.
- **Bundle the extras into one visit.** Fridge, oven, and interior windows added to a scheduled clean cost less than booking them separately.
- **Get flat quotes, not open hourly ones.** For a standard or deep clean, ask for the whole-job price in writing before the visit.
