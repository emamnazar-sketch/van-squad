# How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Salt Lake City?

## The short answer

In Salt Lake City, plan on **$85 to $150 per mobile groom**, tip not included — that's Dog Friendly SLC's figure for this area. Local lists run lower and higher than that band: one Salt Lake operator on that list charges **$70 to $120 by dog size**, another charges a flat **$85 an hour**, and a premium Salt Lake Valley operator starts at **$225 for a tiny dog with no haircut**. Care.com puts an individual Salt Lake City dog groomer at about **$14.85 an hour** — a person, not a van that comes to you.

## What changes the price in Salt Lake City?

**Size is the whole list.** Every Salt Lake list checked prices by weight. Pooch Wagon runs from $225 for a teacup dog with no haircut to $395 for a giant dog with one. Dog Friendly SLC's other operators do the same in a tighter band — $70 to $120, $75 to $145, $80 to $200. A Murray owner with a 20-pound dog and a neighbor with a 90-pound dog aren't buying the same hour of work.

**Where you are in the valley matters.** The directory's Salt Lake City listings all cover one ZIP: Murray, 84107. Operators who drive the whole valley price that drive in. Pooch Wagon serves the entire Salt Lake Valley plus the Wasatch back, from Ogden to Spanish Fork and east to Park City, Heber, and Midway. Dog Friendly SLC lists Aussie Pet Mobile serving Draper, Sandy, and Murray up to North Salt Lake, Woods Cross, and Bountiful. A home on a regular Murray route is an easy slot.

**Coat and time, not just the bath.** Airtasker's guide notes cost depends on coat type, length, and texture, and on whether a coat needs extra work. A flat $85-an-hour list makes that plain: a small dog that fits in the one-hour minimum costs $85, and a bigger or matted dog that takes longer costs more at the same rate. Tell the groomer the breed, weight, and coat state up front — a surprise coat reprices the visit at your door.

**Tip is handled two different ways here.** Dog Friendly SLC puts the usual mobile tip at 10–20% on a card, or $10 or more in cash, and its $85–$150 band leaves tip out. Pooch Wagon builds tip into its flat rate and says there's no need to tip. Ask which way your quote works before the van arrives, not after.

## How to pay less without a worse groom

- **Stay on a standing schedule.** One Salt Lake operator on Dog Friendly SLC's list offers standing appointments from every two weeks up to eight weeks, which keeps a coat in a manageable state — a coat that stays manageable takes less time at a flat hourly rate.
- **Brush between visits.** Airtasker's guide lists at-home maintenance as a way to need fewer full sessions.
- **Skip add-ons your dog doesn't need yet.** If nails and teeth don't need doing this visit, say so — those are priced apart on per-service lists.
- **Get a second dog done in the same visit.** HomeGuide's Salt Lake City page notes prices can be significantly lower for a second and third dog, and Pooch Wagon notes automatic multi-dog discounts. The van is already at your curb.
