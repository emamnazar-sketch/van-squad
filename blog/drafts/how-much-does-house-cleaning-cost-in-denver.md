## What changes the price in Denver?

**Dry air means dust comes back fast.** MaidProfit's Colorado page says it plain: the high altitude and dry climate mean homes here accumulate dust quickly, and that pushes people toward regular cleaning instead of one-off visits. In Hilltop (80220) and Windsor Gardens (80224), that shows up as a biweekly rhythm — Alpine's $245 example home is priced on a two-week cycle, not monthly.

**How the company counts your home matters more than the headline rate.** Alpine Maids prices flat by bedrooms and bathrooms, and says the price only changes on arrival for an obvious problem like a hoarding or post-party cleanup. Angi's Denver pros price by the square foot. Care.com's Denver cleaners price by the hour. Same house, three ways of counting — a 2,400 sq ft home can look cheap by the hour and pricey by the square foot. Get the counting method in writing before you compare two quotes.

**Frequency is the discount.** Kathy Clean's Denver table runs recurring visits from $129 for a small home, while its one-time deep cleans for the same metro run $345 to $835 by size. Mile High Maid, another Denver operator, prices a one-time clean at $155 to $425 and a deep clean at $250 to $430. A first visit costs more because it's doing catch-up work. The second and third visits are the cheaper ones.

**Move-out is its own price tier.** Angi puts a move-out clean for a 1,800 sq ft Denver home at $720, up to $0.40 a square foot, and a pre-sale deep clean at $540 to $720 or more. That's empty-home work — inside cabinets, inside appliances, baseboards a deposit inspection actually checks. Don't budget a move-out from the standard-clean average.

## How to pay less without a worse clean

- **Start recurring, even if it's monthly.** The Denver tables above price repeat visits well under one-time work for the same home.
- **Ask which counting method the quote uses.** Flat by bed and bath, per square foot, and per hour reward different homes. A small home with many bathrooms pays more under bed-and-bath pricing.
- **Name the extras before the visit.** Oven and fridge interiors are deep-clean or add-on work on the Denver lists, not part of a standard visit.
- **Keep the first visit honest.** If the home hasn't been cleaned in months, say so. A crew that arrives expecting a standard clean and finds a deep clean reprices on the spot.
