## What changes the price in Tulsa?

**Size is the first line on every list.** The same full detail is $150 for a car and $200 for a truck or large SUV at Top Tier, and $199 for a sedan against $275 for a third-row vehicle at Tulsa Mobile Detailing Company. A third row is more carpet, glass, and seats to shampoo. Quote it as one — a price given for a sedan won't hold for a three-row.

**The Highway 169 commute is its own kind of dirty.** Tulsa Mobile Detailing Company's Owasso page sells its express exterior as a way to "wash off the Highway 169 commute" — highway miles lay a film of road grit and bug spots across the front of the car that a tunnel wash leaves behind. If your car lives on 169, the exterior is usually the half that needs the work.

**Condition beats package names.** G Car Details prices its $170 full detail for cars "up to about 60% dirty" — past that, expect the quote to move. Pet hair, ground-in snacks in a third row, and red dust in the footwells all take time, and time is the price. Send photos before you book and the number you hear is the number you pay.

**Mobile is sometimes a line item, sometimes built in.** G Car Details charges $20 more to come to you instead of taking a drop-off. Mobile-only operators bake the trip into the package price. Either way, ask whether the detailer brings their own water and power — Tulsa Mobile Detailing Company does, which matters in an apartment lot or office garage in Midtown with no hose hookup nearby.

## How long does a mobile detail take in Tulsa?

Top Tier Detailing puts a full detail at about 2 to 4 hours, at your home or office. That's the real product here — the car gets cleaned in the driveway in ZIP 74114 while you get on with your day. Book the longer end if the car hasn't been detailed in a year; a first detail after a long gap is slower than the same package on a kept-up car.

## How to pay less and still get a real detail

- **Buy the full detail, not the two halves.** On the Tulsa lists above, one combined visit runs $30 to $60 less than the interior and exterior booked apart.
- **Detail the half that is dirty.** If the paint still looks fine, an interior-only visit at $150 to $185 fixes the part you sit in every day. If the inside is clean, a $75 express exterior is the whole job.
- **Name the extras up front.** Pet hair, a child seat, a stained third row — say it on the phone. Surprises found on the driveway are priced on the driveway.
- **Keep it up instead of rescuing it.** A car detailed twice a year stays in the cheaper "up to about 60% dirty" band. One detailed every three years pays rescue prices every time.
