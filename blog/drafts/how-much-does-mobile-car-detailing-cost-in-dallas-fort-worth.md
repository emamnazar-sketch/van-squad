# How much does mobile car detailing cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

In Dallas–Fort Worth, most people pay **$220–$350 for a full mobile detail** (My Detail Guys' Dallas guide). ## What changes the price in Dallas–Fort Worth?

**Vehicle size, first.** DFW runs on trucks and big SUVs, and every list prices that in. My Detail Guys charges $279 for a car and $329 for a large SUV, van or truck for the same full detail. Gilded charges $300 for a car and $450 for a three-row. More seats, more carpet, more paint. Simple math.

**Texas heat and sun.** Panda Hub's Dallas page is blunt about leather: cabin heat can crack it in a single summer here, so conditioning is part of a Dallas full detail, not a nice extra. The same page ties paint damage to construction grit on I-35 and I-635 commutes — fine debris working into the clear coat on a car parked outside in Lower Greenville (75206) or Frisco (75034).

**Pet hair, stains and sand.** My Detail Guys names pet hair as a top price driver in Dallas, and sand in carpets as a common Texas problem. On its Fort Worth list, heavy pet hair and spill removal starts at $50 extra, a clay bar is $75 and headlight work is $99. Name the mess before you book and the quote holds.

**Water and power at your spot.** Dallas Best, My Detail Guys and Panda Hub all say they bring their own. That matters in DFW — an apartment lot in Lower Greenville or an office garage in Frisco may have no hookup at all.

## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Keep up a cheaper wash between full details.** A $79.91 express in Dallas or a $99 Deluxe Wash in Fort Worth holds a car that already had a full detail. It does not replace one.
- **Send photos for the quote.** My Detail Guys asks for daylight photos of the seats, carpets, trunk and wheels. A photo quote is a firm quote. A guess is not.
- **Skip the top package on a car you are selling soon.** The jump from $279 to $379 buys iron removal and a longer sealant. Worth it on a car you keep. Hard to get back on a trade-in.
- **Book the interior only if the paint is fine.** Interior work in Dallas mostly lands at $150–$220 for a mid-size car. No need to pay full-detail money for a cabin problem.
