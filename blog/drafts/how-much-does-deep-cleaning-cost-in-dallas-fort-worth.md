## What changes the price in Dallas–Fort Worth?

**Size, first.** Angi prices Dallas by the square foot: deep cleaning about $0.25 per square foot against $0.10–$0.20 for a standard clean. The median Dallas home is about 2,200 sq ft, which is how you get to $550. A 1,200 sq ft Arlington apartment is a different job entirely.

**Texas dust and new construction.** Dallas Maids flags it plainly: dust gets into Dallas homes even with the windows closed, and new construction runs from Frisco down through Dallas. Dust settles on blinds, baseboards and ceiling fans — exactly what a deep clean is priced to clear.

**Tile, grout and muggy summers.** Tile floors are common in Dallas–Fort Worth kitchens and bathrooms, and grout scrubbing is slow work. Thumbtack's Dallas guidance adds that muggy summers can bring bathroom mold — slower to deal with in older Lake Highlands (75243) homes than in a new build.

**How long since the last deep clean.** A first visit is priced like a reset, because it is one. Grease and buildup take time the first time through. After that, the home holds with standard visits at $140–$280.

## Deep clean or standard clean — what does the extra money buy?

A standard clean covers the regular rooms: kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, floors, dusting. A deep clean adds the buildup — baseboards, grout, ceiling fans, blinds and window tracks, and behind and under furniture and appliances.

That gap is the $140–$280 standard range against $220–$440 for deep on Dallas Maids' list. If your home gets a regular clean already, you probably do not need the deep price. If nobody has touched the baseboards or grout in a year, a standard clean will not catch you up.

Inside the oven, fridge and cabinets are add-ons with many companies. Name them before the visit, in writing, or expect them to be extra.

## How to pay less and still get a real deep clean

- **Book the deep clean once, then maintain it.** Pay the deep price for the reset, then drop to standard visits — no reset prices twice a year.
- **Clear the clutter before they arrive.** Time spent moving toys and mail is time not spent on grout.
- **Point the visit at kitchens and bathrooms.** That is where the buildup lives. Bedrooms can stay standard if money is tight.
- **Book ahead of the rush.** Thumbtack notes spring cleaning season and the weeks before major holidays get packed in Dallas — booking early gets you the slot and sometimes a better price.
