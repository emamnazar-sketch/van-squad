# How much does a handyman cost in Kansas City, Kansas?

## The short answer

In Kansas City, Kansas, a handyman runs **$50 to $90 an hour** on HomeBlue's Kansas City, Kansas page, materials on top. A half day (4 hours) runs **$180 to $350**, and a full day (8 hours) **$370 to $690**. Small jobs often price flat instead: on Thumbtack's Overland Park page, a toilet replacement, a ceiling fan or light fixture, and a garbage disposal each run **$75 flat**, a faucet replacement **$125**, and minimum charges sit at **$60 to $100**.

## How to pay less and still get the work done

- **Write the whole list before you call.** Doors, patches, fixtures, the lot. One visit, one minimum, one trip instead of three.
- **Ask which jobs price flat.** A toilet at $75 flat and a faucet at $125 flat can beat an open hourly clock.
- **Supply the parts when you can.** HomeBlue's prices are labor — materials sit on top. A fixture already in the house saves a shopping trip billed as time.
- **Stay flexible on timing.** HomeBlue lists flexible scheduling and one-day jobs as things that pull a price down, and emergency calls and permit work as things that push it up.
