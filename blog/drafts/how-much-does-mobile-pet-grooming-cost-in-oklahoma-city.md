# How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Oklahoma City?

## The short answer

HomeGuide's Oklahoma City page puts a mobile full service — ears, nails, glands, bath, blow dry, and scissor finish — at **about $75 for an average-size dog**. Thumbtack's Oklahoma City page prices that same bundle higher, at **$110–$160 depending on your dog's size**. A salon groom costs less: full service starts around **$40 for a small dog and $75 for a large dog** (HomeGuide). The extra pays for the van in your driveway, and a groomer on just your dog.

Those two mobile numbers look far apart, and this is where people overpay. The $75 is one average-size dog. The $110–$160 is a bundle across sizes, so a big dog sits at the top of it. Check what's in a quote, and what size dog it priced, before you compare.

Care.com is a different kind of price: individual groomers in Oklahoma City at an average posted $12.00 an hour as of October 2026. That's a person by the hour, not a van visit. Nationally, Thumbtack puts dog grooming at **$60–$80** across standard and mobile services — a national figure, not an Oklahoma City one.

## What changes the price in Oklahoma City?

**Size and coat, again.** Your dog sets the price. A local salon list shows it plainly: at The Paw Spa, serving Oklahoma City, Mustang, and Moore, a full groom starts at $70 — but poodles and doodles start at $85 for a toy or small dog and run to $175 for an extra-large one. Curly coats take longer. Mats take longer.

**Where you sit on the route.** Van Squads' Oklahoma City mobile groomers cluster on the north side — Warr Acres and northwest Oklahoma City (73162) and up into Edmond (73003, 73012, 73013, 73025, 73034). A regular route day in Edmond, Deer Creek, or North OKC is easy to fit in. A one-off trip across the metro is a harder slot to get.

**Oklahoma weather on a coat.** Red dirt after a storm, burrs from the yard, and a winter coat blowing out in spring all add time. A dog groomed every 4–6 weeks is a short visit. A dog left for three months is not.

## How to pay less without a worse groom

- **Stay on a 4–6 week schedule.** Shorter visits cost less than rescue grooms, and you keep a regular slot on the route.
- **Brush between visits.** A few minutes at home skips extra de-matting time at the van.
- **Ask about the second dog.** HomeGuide notes prices can be significantly lower for a second and third dog in the same stop — the van is already at your curb.
- **Compare like for like.** Line up what's included before you call one quote cheaper than another.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Oklahoma City?**
About $75 for a mobile full service for an average-size dog on HomeGuide's Oklahoma City page, and $110–$160 for a bundled bath, haircut, nails, ears, and glands package by dog size on Thumbtack's Oklahoma City page. Size and coat are what move you inside that range.

**Why does mobile grooming cost more than a salon in Oklahoma City?**
The groomer brings the salon to you — van, water, power, and one-on-one time with just your dog — and drives between homes. Salon full service starts around $40 for a small dog (HomeGuide), and a local salon full groom starts at $70 (The Paw Spa).

**What is included in a full mobile groom?**
Bath, blow dry, haircut or scissor finish, nail trim, ear cleaning, and gland expression — the bundle both HomeGuide and Thumbtack describe. Add-ons like teeth brushing and flea treatment cost extra, usually $10–$15 each on HomeGuide's Oklahoma City list.

**How much should I tip a mobile dog groomer in Oklahoma City?**
15 to 20 percent of the service total, per HomeGuide's Oklahoma City page. Tip more if your dog was heavily matted, very dirty, or hard to handle — that was extra work.
