# How much does mobile car detailing cost in Oklahoma City?

## The short answer

Most Oklahoma City drivers pay **$150 for a basic mobile detail, $250 for a full detail, and $300 for a premium detail**, done in the driveway (All Hands On Dash, Oklahoma City). If you only need one side of the car done, an exterior-only or interior-only detail runs **$90 for a car and $105 for an SUV or truck** (ARC Mobile Detailing, Oklahoma City).

## What changes the price in Oklahoma City?

**Vehicle size.** This is pickup and three-row SUV country, and size is priced in everywhere. ARC charges $15 more for an SUV or truck on its single-side and complete details. All Hands On Dash adds a flat $50 for larger vehicles on its detail tiers. A Good prices the same in-and-out package at $167 for a small car and $249 for a big truck or SUV.

**Condition, mostly the interior.** Pet hair, stains, and odor work are where quotes jump. A Good starts pet hair removal at $65 and prices stain extraction for four seats at $100 on top of the package. If your back seat carries kids and a dog daily, send photos before you book — a photo quote beats a surprise charge in your driveway.

**Water and power — Oklahoma City detailers split on this, so ask.** Some bring everything with them. All Hands On Dash says it typically arrives ready to work, and Revive405 says it comes fully equipped for houses, apartments, and offices. ARC asks you to provide water access and one standard electrical outlet. If you're in an apartment near downtown (73102) or booking at an office lot with no hookup, that one question decides who can take the job.

## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Buy the side that's actually dirty.** A clean exterior with a rough cabin needs the $90 interior detail, not a $250 full package.
- **Skip correction on a car you're selling soon.** The jump from ARC's $180 complete detail to its $325 signature detail is mostly machine polishing. It pays back on a car you keep, not one going up for sale next month.
- **Price the add-ons before the visit.** Pet hair, headlights, and engine work are all line items here. Agree on them up front and your final bill matches the quote.
