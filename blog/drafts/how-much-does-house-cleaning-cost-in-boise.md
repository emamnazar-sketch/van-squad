In Boise, most standard homes are cleaned for **$120 to $250 per visit** (Scrub 'N Bubbles; MaidProfit agrees). A cleaner starts at **$20.38 an hour** on Care.com as of October 2026. The hourly gap trips people up. Care.com's $20.38 is a starting rate for cleaners listed there. Company crews run $26 to $42 an hour per cleaner (MaidProfit). Not the same thing — do not expect a company quote to match the lower number.

This is where people overpay: paying a deep-clean price for a home that needs a standard visit, or booking one-time cleans over and over. Recurring service is 10 to 15% cheaper per visit than one-time cleans (Lone Star).

## What changes the price in Boise?

**Home size and bathrooms.** Every Boise list prices this first. Wanny Clean goes from $95 a week for 1,200 sq ft to $375 a week at 5,400 sq ft. Bathrooms add detail work on top.

**Which Boise home you have.** Older houses in the North End (83703) and East End have original woodwork, deep ledges and wood floors that need a careful hand. Newer homes in Harris Ranch (83716) and Southeast Boise (83706) mean open kitchens, several bathrooms and big windows. Ranch homes on the Bench (83705) and in West Boise (83704, 83713) bring carpet, tile and yard traffic. Same city, different jobs.

**First clean vs. recurring.** Most Boise cleaners want a deep clean first. Wanny Clean prices its Welcome Clean separately, after it learns the home's size, condition and pets. After that, weekly is the lowest price per visit — less builds up.

**Pets and mudrooms.** Pet hair adds time, and mudrooms and garages track in dirt all winter. Name both before you book and the quote holds.

**Where you sit in the Treasure Valley.** Care.com puts Meridian at $22.74 an hour starting, above Boise's $20.38, with Eagle at $21.45 and Nampa at $20.08. A Meridian (83642, 83646) quote a bit above a Boise one is normal, not a markup.

## How to pay less without getting a worse clean

- **Go every other week if weekly feels like too much.** A 2,400 sq ft home is $235 every other week (Wanny Clean). Lone Star calls bi-weekly the most popular Treasure Valley choice too.
- **Start recurring after the deep clean, not instead of it.** The deep clean does baseboards, cabinet fronts and buildup once. Paying standard money and hoping for deep-clean work leaves both sides annoyed.
- **Get a flat price in writing.** Square footage, bedrooms and bathrooms are enough to quote flat. An open hourly quote on a big Harris Ranch home can drift.
- **Skip add-ons you do not need.** Inside the oven, inside the fridge and wall washing are priced separately in Boise. Add one when it needs doing.
