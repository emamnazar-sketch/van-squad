# How much does mobile car detailing cost in Sacramento?

## The short answer

In Sacramento, mobile car detailing runs from about **$88 for an express detail** to **$250–$330 for a full detail**, done in your driveway. One Sacramento mobile detailer's sedan packages run **$150, $180, $250, and $370** by level, each taking about 1.5 to 4 hours. SUVs and trucks cost more than sedans at every level, and a car that needs stain, odor, or pet-hair work costs more than a car in good shape.

## What changes the price in Sacramento?

**Vehicle size and condition.** Sacramento packages are quoted for sedans first. A three-row SUV Elk Grove family hauler has more glass, more carpet, and more paint than a Midtown commuter sedan, and every detailer above prices that in. Pet hair, stains, and odors are the other big mover — one Sacramento interior specialist lists a full interior detail from $230, with mold and odor work starting at $460.

**Water and power — ask before you book.** Sacramento mobile detailers split on this, and it decides whether they can work at your home or office at all. Nick's Premium brings its own water and power. Mud2Mirror, which details within 20 miles of Sacramento, connects to your water and electricity on site — and takes $25 off if you drop the car at its shop instead. An apartment in Downtown (95814) or an office lot without a hookup rules out the second kind of detailer.

**Package level, not time.** The jump from $150 to $370 on the same sedan is what is in the package: wheels, tires, and glass at the second level; paint and interior protection at the third; engine bay, iron removal, and water-spot removal at the top. Paying for the top package on a car you are about to sell rarely pays back — the middle packages are the sweet spot for a car you keep.

## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Join a maintenance plan after one full detail.** Sacramento plans run $75–$99 a visit once the car is already clean — far less than re-buying a full detail twice a year.
- **Match the package to the problem.** A clean car with dull paint needs the protection level, not the top restoration level. A dirty interior with clean paint needs an interior detail, not a full package.
- **Send photos for an exact quote.** Sacramento detailers ask for photos of the car's size and condition before quoting specialty work — a photo quote beats a surprise upcharge in your driveway.
