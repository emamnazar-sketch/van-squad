## What changes the price in Sioux Falls?

**Size first, condition second.** Precision starts a full detail at $330 for a car and $400 for a large SUV, before it even looks at the state of the thing. Its final price is set after a pre-inspection, and extra fees can apply for pet hair, stains, mud, and bugs. Freedom lists everything as "starting at" for the same reason. A garage-kept sedan and a work truck that hauls gravel out past Tea are not the same job.

**Winter is the local bill.** Sioux Falls is heading into salt and slush season. Salt sits on lower panels and eats into mats and carpet all winter. A $120 wash and wax now costs less than correction work in March. One Sioux Falls detailer, HD Detailing, puts it plainly in a customer quote on its site: after a long winter in South Dakota, the car needed cleaning inside and out.

**Mobile or shop changes what you are buying.** Freedom comes to your driveway or office in Sioux Falls, Tea, or Brandon, and its wash starts at $99. Precision's full detail runs $330–$400 because it includes a one-step polish and a 3–6 month wax. Two different jobs, not two prices for one job.

**Add-ons are where a $275 detail becomes a $400 one.** Headlight restoration is $89 at Freedom and trim restoration is $49. Precision charges $75 for an engine bay and $50 for harsh odor removal, which may still need a full interior detail on top. Price all of it before the work starts, not after.

## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Buy the half that is dirty.** Interior-only starts at $199 at Freedom. Exterior-only starts at $180 at Precision, and a wash and wax is $120. A full detail is the wrong buy when only one side needs work.
- **Send photos before you book.** Every list here prices by condition. Photos of the seats, mats, and worst panel get you a real number instead of a starting number.
- **Ask who brings water and power.** Some mobile detailers run self-contained units, others need your hose and outlet. In an apartment lot or an office garage, that decides who can take the job at all.
