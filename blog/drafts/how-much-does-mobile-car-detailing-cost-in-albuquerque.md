# How much does mobile car detailing cost in Albuquerque?

## The short answer

In Albuquerque, mobile car detailing runs from about **$125 for an interior-only clean** to **$370–$475 for a full detail**, done in your driveway. A basic inside-and-out detail for a sedan is **$150–$175** at published local prices. SUVs and trucks cost more at every level, and the top package — paint correction and a ceramic coating — runs **$740 for a sedan, $890 for an SUV or truck**.

## What changes the price in Albuquerque?

**Desert dust and hard water.** Fine dust gets into vents, cupholders, and carpet here, and Albuquerque tap water leaves mineral spots when a rinse dries in the sun. That is why Enchanted Detail Co carries deionized water and runs off its own power rig — and says plainly that is why its prices sit above the typical mobile detailer in town. A spigot-water rinse on a sunny Northeast Heights driveway can bake spots into paint just cleaned.

**Sun and heat.** The high-desert sun fades untreated leather and cracks dashboards. Platinum's interior package includes dash UV protection for that reason. If your car lives outside in 87111 or 87122, that protection is not an upsell.

**Vehicle size and condition.** Every local list prices sedans first. A three-row SUV has more glass, carpet, and paint, so Enchanted's basic detail goes from $160 to $185, and the full detail from $370 to $455. Pet hair is extra on Platinum's packages, and dog hair or stains take longer than the package time on the page.

**Water and power — ask before you book.** Platinum brings its own water and power, and Enchanted runs off its own rig. Not every mobile detailer does. If yours needs your hose and an outlet, a parking spot with no hookup can stop the job before it starts.

## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Keep it up on a plan after one full detail.** Atomic Fleet & Mobile Detail's Albuquerque monthly plan starts at $145 a month for a sedan and $175 for an SUV or truck — less than re-buying a full detail twice a year.
- **Buy only the half that is dirty.** A clean outside with a rough cabin is an interior job — $125 at MobiGleam, from $195 at Platinum. A clean cabin with dull paint is an exterior job, from $145 at Platinum. No full package needed for a one-sided problem.
- **Skip the showroom tier on a car you are not selling.** The $740–$890 top tier is paint correction and a 3-year coating. Real work, wrong purchase for a daily driver that just needs the dust and dog hair out.
