# How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?

## The short answer

In Dallas–Fort Worth, a mobile full groom runs **$78–$105 for a small dog, $105–$145 for a medium dog, $132–$178 for a large dog, and $165–$222 for an XL dog** (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming, 2026). A bath and brush costs less — **$65–$88 small, $88–$118 medium, $112–$148 large**. Doodles sit higher: **$118–$188 for a full groom**. The van in your driveway and one-on-one time are what you pay extra for over a salon.

## What changes the price in Dallas–Fort Worth?

**Size and coat, first.** The table is really a time table. A large double-coated dog takes longer to wash, dry, and brush out than a short-coated small dog, and a doodle costs more than a same-size short coat because its coat keeps growing and mats.

**Texas heat.** Summer heat runs May through September here, with early morning scheduling prioritized (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming). In a Garland or Arlington August, the good slots go first. Book the morning.

**How far out you are.** Pricing is consistent across most of the metroplex, and a travel surcharge may apply to far outer suburbs. Van Squads' coverage shows why: listed groomers cluster in Arlington (76018), Garland (75044), and Lewisville (75067). Inside a cluster you are a route stop. Well past it, you are a special trip.

**Mats and add-ons are paid time.** On America's Paw Spaw's list, de-shedding starts at $25 and matting starts at $40, with nail filing at $15 and oral care at $10. A matted doodle in Mesquite is not the $118 starting price anymore.

Most breeds do fine every 6–8 weeks. Poodles, doodles, and other coats that keep growing need it every 4–6 weeks. Kept on that schedule, a dog is a routine visit at the table price. Left three months and matted, the same dog pays more and takes longer.

## How to pay less without a worse groom

- **Take the bath and brush between haircuts.** At $65–$118 for small and medium dogs, it keeps the full groom routine.
- **Stay on a 4–6 week schedule for doodles and long coats.** Shorter visits cost less than rescue grooms.
- **Brush between visits.** Mats start at $40 on a local list — a few minutes at home avoids that fee.
- **Compare what is included.** One quote bundles nails, ears, and glands; another adds them at $10–$25 each.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Dallas–Fort Worth?**
$78–$105 for a small dog, $105–$145 for a medium dog, $132–$178 for a large dog, and $165–$222 for an XL dog for a full groom (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming, 2026). Bath and brush with no haircut runs $65–$182 by size, and doodles run $118–$188 for a full groom.

**How does mobile dog grooming work in Dallas–Fort Worth?**
A groomer drives a fitted-out van to your home and grooms your dog in the van at your driveway — no cage, no waiting room, no other animals. Most appointments take 1–2 hours, depending on breed and size (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming).

**Is mobile grooming better for an anxious dog?**
For a lot of dogs, yes. One-on-one, cage-free grooming removes the car ride, the cage wait, and the noise from other animals — the parts that stress anxious dogs most at a salon (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming).

**How often should I groom my dog in Dallas–Fort Worth?**
Every 6–8 weeks for most breeds. Poodles, doodles, and other coats that keep growing need every 4–6 weeks, and heavy shedders may need de-shedding more often (DFW Mobile Dog Grooming).
