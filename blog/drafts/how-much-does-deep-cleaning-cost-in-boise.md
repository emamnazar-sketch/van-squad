## What changes the price in Boise?

**Size and bedrooms, first.** Every Treasure Valley list prices this way: Lone Star goes from $180–$260 for 1–2 bedrooms to $400 or more at 5-plus. Bathrooms add scrubbing time on top.

**How long since the last real clean.** Favorite Day Maid says it plainly: a home that has not had a professional clean in 6 or more months runs higher, and so do larger homes and post-construction cleans. A first deep clean in a West Boise (83704) ranch that has gone a year is priced like a reset, because it is one.

**Dust, not mold.** Idaho's dry climate means less mold but more dust buildup (MaidProfit). Dust settles on blinds, ceiling fans, vents and baseboards — exactly the list a deep clean is priced to clear.

**Time on site.** A deep clean takes 4 to 8 hours (Favorite Day Maid). A 1,500–2,000 sq ft home with two cleaners usually takes 4–5 hours; a 3-bedroom home takes 4–6 hours with a two-person team (Lone Star). More hours, more money.

**Add-ons.** Inside the oven, fridge and cabinets are add-ons or small-fee extras on Treasure Valley lists, not standard. Name them before the visit or expect them as extras.

## Deep clean or standard clean — what does the extra money buy?

A standard clean handles the regular rooms: counters, bathrooms, floors, dusting the visible surfaces. Recurring visits in the Treasure Valley run $120–$280 depending on size (Lone Star).

A deep clean adds the buildup: hand-washed baseboards, doors and door frames, window sills and tracks, light fixtures, ceiling fans, blinds, vents, cabinet fronts and a deeper bathroom scrub (Lone Star; Favorite Day Maid's list is much the same).

If your home already gets a regular clean, you probably do not need the deep price. If nobody has touched the baseboards in a year, a standard clean will not catch you up.

## How to pay less and still get a real deep clean

- **Book the deep clean once, then keep it up.** Pay the reset price once, then drop to recurring visits, which run 10–15% below one-time prices (Lone Star).
- **Clear the clutter before they arrive.** Favorite Day Maid asks clients to declutter surfaces first — time spent moving mail and toys is time not spent on grout.
- **Ask about a recurring discount on the first visit.** One Eagle company knocks $100 off the deep cleaning when you schedule weekly or every-other-week service after it (Favorite Day Maid). Worth asking any company you quote.
- **Point the visit at kitchens and bathrooms.** That is where the buildup lives. Bedrooms can stay standard if money is tight.
