# How Much Does a Deep Clean Cost?

## The short answer

In 2026, a professional deep clean costs **$260 on average**, with most homeowners paying **$180–$375**. Cleaners typically charge **$0.10–$0.30 per square foot** or **$25–$70 per hour**. Your home's size is the biggest price driver — a small apartment can run $100–$200 while a large home can reach $900.

## What does a deep clean cost in 2026?

Here's how pricing breaks down by home size:

| Home size | Typical 2026 price |
|---|---|
| Up to 800 sq ft (apartment/condo) | $100–$200 |
| 800–1,500 sq ft (standard home) | $140–$320 |
| 1,500–2,500 sq ft (large home) | $245–$575 |
| 2,500+ sq ft (very large/estate) | $330–$900 |

Those ranges cover a standard deep clean — the whole-home, top-to-bottom reset. Specialized deep cleans cost more: move-in/move-out runs $140–$500, allergen removal $210–$440, pet-focused treatments $190–$475, and post-construction cleanup $340–$800.

## What's the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A regular clean is maintenance — the visible stuff, done often. A deep clean goes after built-up grime: baseboards, behind and under appliances, light fixtures, window tracks, grout lines, and every surface that's been collecting dust for months. That's why it costs 2–3x a standard visit and takes much longer. Most homes need one every 3–6 months, with regular cleanings in between.

## What drives the price up or down?

- **Home size.** The #1 factor. More square feet means more time, more product, more labor — most companies quote by square footage ($0.10–$0.30) or by room ($25–$60 per room).
- **Type of deep clean.** A standard whole-home deep clean ($180–$375) costs less than post-construction ($340–$800) or allergen removal ($210–$440), which need specialty equipment and products.
- **Condition.** A home that hasn't been deep cleaned in two years takes far longer than one done every six months. Heavy buildup, pet damage, and mold all add time — and cost.
- **Location.** Major metro areas run higher; small towns and suburbs cost less. Some cleaners add travel fees outside their standard service area, and many set a $100–$150 minimum.
- **Add-ons.** Carpet or upholstery cleaning ($75–$200 per room), interior windows ($3–$15 each), and inside-appliance cleaning ($10–$50 per appliance) are usually priced separately.

## How can I pay less for a deep clean?

- **Declutter before they arrive.** Every minute a cleaner spends tidying is a minute not spent deep cleaning — clear surfaces, gather laundry, and move small furniture.
- **Book recurring service.** Many companies discount repeat visits 10–30%. A deep clean twice a year plus regular maintenance often costs less over time than one rescue clean.
- **Focus on what matters.** Ask for high-traffic areas and problem rooms only, instead of the whole house, if budget is tight.
- **Bundle services.** Carpet cleaning, window washing, or organizing booked together often comes with package pricing.
- **Compare multiple quotes.** Prices vary widely between cleaners — get at least three and compare what's actually included, not just the headline number.

## What should I ask before booking?

1. **What's included in the base price — and what counts as an add-on?** Get the room-by-room checklist, not the sales pitch.
2. **How do you price — square footage, hourly, or per room?** Each method favors different homes; know which one you're getting.
3. **Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?** Standard products are usually included; confirm before you buy anything yourself.
4. **What happens if I'm not happy with the result?** A confident cleaner has a clear re-clean policy.
5. **What's your cancellation policy?** Deep cleans block out half a day — know the terms before you book.

## FAQ

**How much should I tip for a deep clean?**
Tipping is customary but not required — 10–20% of the total is standard when you're pleased with the work. Check your invoice first; some companies include gratuity.

**How often should I deep clean my house?**
Every 3–6 months for most homes. Homes with pets, kids, allergy sufferers, or smokers may need it monthly or even biweekly.

**What's usually not included in a deep clean?**
Carpet and upholstery shampooing ($75–$200 per room), interior window washing ($3–$15 per window), and cleaning inside appliances ($10–$50 each) are typical add-ons — confirm before booking.

**Why is my quote higher than the $260 average?**
The average covers standard homes in average condition. Bigger homes, heavy buildup, metro-area labor rates, and add-ons like carpet or pet treatments all push quotes above it.

**Do cleaners bring their own supplies?**
Usually yes — standard products and equipment are included in the quote. Eco-friendly or specialty products can add $10–$100 per visit.
