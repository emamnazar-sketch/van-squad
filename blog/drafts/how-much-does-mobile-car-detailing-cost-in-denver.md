## What changes the price in Denver?

**Altitude sun works on your paint all year.** Denver Auto Shield, a Denver protection shop, puts it bluntly: Denver is a mile closer to the sun, with roughly 300 days of sunshine a year, and that UV load oxidizes clear coat and fades pigment. That's why protection shows up on so many Denver lists — Creek prices paint enhancement from $349 and ceramic protection from $699, and Euro Mile High starts paint correction at $329. On a car you plan to keep, that work has a point here. On one you are selling this spring, it usually does not.

**Winter leaves a film you can see.** Denver Auto Shield says winter roads here get covered in rocks and salt and drenched in mag chloride, and people clean their cars less in the cold, so the stuff sits and bonds to the paint. Jazz It Up, in Centennial, calls the residue a filmy white spray down the doors and rocker panels, and says to wash with pH-balanced soap after mag-chloride exposure. A spring detail in University Park (80210) or Saddle Rock in Aurora (80016) is often priced by how much of that film has to come off first.

**Size first, then condition.** All In's ladder is plain: $150, $200, $250 by size for the same job. PEAK runs $110 to $170 for an interior by size. Creek says size, condition, and add-on adjustments are quoted before service, and heavy pet hair or staining can mean an added charge.

**Pet hair is priced three different ways in this metro.** All In adds a flat $50 on its basic and interior-and-exterior packages, then includes removal in its premium package. PEAK lists it at $20. Creek quotes it case by case. Same dog, three prices — ask which rule your detailer uses before you book.

**Who brings water and power decides where the job can happen.** Creek says its setup is self-contained for most appointments, and it will tell you before booking if a job needs your water or power. PEAK says it may need an outdoor hookup and an outlet, confirmed before the appointment. In an apartment lot in University Park, that difference decides the booking.

## How to pay less without a worse detail

- **Buy the half that's dirty.** Interior-only starts at $100–$149 on the lists above, exterior-only at $90–$129. A full detail is the wrong buy when one side is fine.
- **Name the pet hair up front.** It's $20 at PEAK, $50 at All In, and a custom quote at Creek. Naming it early keeps the quote you were given.
- **Time the big detail for spring.** Getting the winter film off early is cleaning work. Leaving it bonded another season turns it into correction work, and correction starts at $329–$349.
- **Ask about water and power before you book.** Tell the detailer what your driveway or lot has.
