## What changes the price in Omaha?

**Winter salt is the big one.** Concrete drives, walks, and patios in Omaha collect salt residue all winter, then spring pollen, then summer mildew (That 1 Painter, Omaha–Council Bluffs). A driveway washed every spring is a short job. One that sat through two winters of salt needs pre-treatment and a second pass.

**Humid summers grow a green film.** Omaha summers are humid, and mildew and algae build up on shaded sides of a house — a green film on siding, black streaks on a roof (No Pressure Power Washing). That growth is soft wash work: solution and dwell time, not just water.

**Old brick doesn't take a hard blast.** Omaha's historic brick homes in Dundee need pressure matched to the masonry so aged mortar isn't harmed (That 1 Painter). The same crew soft-washes stucco two-stories in Bemis Park and aluminum-sided ranches in Ralston and Millard, and saves high pressure for concrete.

**Bigger house, bigger bill.** Brush & Roll puts Omaha house washing at $250–$500 depending on home size, surface material, and how much cleaning the exterior needs. A two-story house in Midtown (68105) with tight side yards costs more than a one-story ranch with open access.

## Should your house be pressure washed or soft washed?

Concrete driveways and walkways get high pressure. Painted siding and stucco get a soft wash — lower pressure with cleaning solution (That 1 Painter, Omaha–Council Bluffs).

Hot water is the other upsell to check. HomeGuide puts plain pressure washing at $0.08–$0.20 per sq ft nationally, and power washing — heated water — at $0.35–$0.77 nationally. Hot water earns its price on greasy concrete. On plain salt film and pollen, it usually doesn't.

## How to pay less and still get it clean

- **Bundle surfaces in one visit.** Window Genie of Omaha advertises 10% multi-service discounts on its Omaha price page. House, driveway, and walkway in one stop beats three separate trips.
- **Wash in spring, after salt season.** The salt is already on the concrete by March. One spring wash clears the whole winter. A fall wash just gets salted over again.
- **Price the rental honestly.** Renting a power washer in Omaha runs about $40–$80 a day (Brush & Roll). Cheap for a driveway. A bad trade on siding — the wrong pressure etches concrete, strips paint, and can force water behind siding.
- **Get the cap in writing.** If a quote says "up to 2,500 sq ft," ask what your house measures and what the price becomes if it's over.
