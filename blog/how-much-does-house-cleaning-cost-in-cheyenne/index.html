How much does house cleaning cost in Cheyenne?

The short answer. A standard house cleaning in Cheyenne runs $108 to $230 a visit on MaidProfit's Wyoming page, which carries a Cheyenne row. Per hour, Cheyenne cleaners run $23 to $40 an hour per cleaner on that same page, and Care.com puts Wyoming's posted rate at $20.45 an hour. A BLS-based pricing engine puts a typical Cheyenne clean at $124 to $168. The trap is the wind. Dust gets into window tracks and on top of door frames between visits, so a cheap quote that skips those spots is not the same clean.

Read the hourly rows in two groups. MaidProfit's $23 to $40 and Care.com's $20.45 are what cleaners post and charge here. BIG WORKS' $40 to $50 row is not a survey — it takes a BLS Cheyenne wage and multiplies it to cover a company's overhead. Both are real numbers. This is where people overpay in Cheyenne. They book a one-time clean at a one-time price, every time. MaidProfit's Wyoming recurring rows sit below its standard range — weekly at $90 to $205 against $105 to $240 — because a home on a rhythm takes less work per visit. A home rescued twice a year costs more each time, and here the rescue visit carries months of wind-blown dust with it.

Why does Cheyenne dust change what a clean has to cover?

Cheyenne is one of the windiest cities in the country, and the wind carries fine dust off the plains straight into your home. A local cleaning guide puts it plainly: that dust settles in places you rarely look — on top of door frames, inside window tracks, behind heavy furniture. Homeaglow's Cheyenne page gives the rhythm that follows: a deep clean every 3 to 6 months, because the gusts keep loading those spots back up.

So ask what a quote covers before you compare two of them. A clean that includes window tracks and the tops of frames is a different job from one that stops at counters, floors, and bathrooms. In a less windy city that gap is small. In Cheyenne it is the whole argument.

What does coverage in ZIP 82001 actually cover?

Our directory's Cheyenne house cleaning coverage sits in ZIP 82001, the middle of the city — Downtown, Capitol North, Rainsford, Lakeview, and the streets around Holliday Park. Masters' Guild's Cheyenne page names the same ground: Capitol North, Moore Haven, Rainsford, the Dubois Block, Lakeview, and Downtown Cheyenne.

A lot of 82001 is old housing stock. The Capitol North Historic District, just north of the State Capitol, was built between 1905 and 1930 — frame cottages with clapboard siding, brick cottages, trim and small details that came in by railroad. Old trim holds dust. Narrow window tracks in a 1915 cottage collect more of it than a new build's do, and the plaster and woodwork want a gentler hand than a newer ranch on the edge of town. Same ZIP, same hourly rate — the older house is the longer visit.

Should you hire an individual or a company in Cheyenne?

An individual posting on Care.com runs around $20 an hour in Wyoming. A company bills more — BIG WORKS' modeled Cheyenne range is $40 to $50 an hour — because the rate carries insurance, supplies, and cover when someone calls in sick. HomeGuide's national split says the same thing: independents at $15 to $40 an hour, companies at $25 to $70.

Neither pick is wrong. An individual is cheaper if you have found someone steady and you are home to let them in. A company is safer if you want the visit to happen even when one person is out. Just do not hold a company's per-visit quote up against an individual's hourly post and call the company a rip-off. Run both numbers out to a full visit first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a house cleaner charge per hour in Cheyenne?

MaidProfit puts Cheyenne at $23 to $40 an hour per cleaner. Care.com puts Wyoming's posted rate at $20.45 an hour, just under its $21.29 national average. A company billing for overhead runs higher — BIG WORKS' modeled Cheyenne rate is $40 to $50 an hour, built from the local wage, not quoted from jobs.

How much is a standard house cleaning visit in Cheyenne?

$108 to $230 a visit on MaidProfit's Cheyenne row. Masters' Guild's Cheyenne page — US ranges adjusted for the city's price level, its own disclosure — puts a standard clean at $120 to $270, and a BLS-based engine expects $124 to $168. HomeGuide's national range is $120 to $280 a visit, labeled national — Cheyenne sits inside it, toward the low end.

How much does a deep clean or a move-out clean cost in Cheyenne?

MaidProfit's Wyoming rows put a deep cleaning at $230 to $450 and a move-out at $255 to $490. Masters' Guild's adjusted Cheyenne figures run higher: $190 to $480 deep, $240 to $580 move-out. A move-out costs more because the home is empty and the standard is different: inside the oven, inside the fridge, cabinets wiped out, ready for a landlord's walk-through.

How often should a Cheyenne home get a deep clean?

Every 3 to 6 months, on Homeaglow's Cheyenne page — its reason is the local one: frequent gusts kick up dust, and it builds up in the hard-to-reach spots a standard clean does not touch. A Cheyenne guide names the same spots: window tracks, door-frame tops, behind heavy furniture.

Should you tip a house cleaner in Cheyenne?

Thumbtack's Cheyenne page says tipping between 15 and 20 percent is considered appropriate for maids and house cleaners. On a $150 visit, that is $22 to $30. Plenty of Cheyenne homeowners skip the math and round a regular cleaner's visit up in cash — what matters is that a steady cleaner hears the thanks in the pay, not just in a note on the counter.
