How Much Does a Handyman Cost in Sacramento?

The short answer. In Sacramento, a handyman costs about $50 to $100 an hour (HomeBlue). A half day — 4 hours — runs $210 to $400, plus materials, and a full 8-hour day runs $420 to $790. Sacramento listings on HomeAdvisor show general rates of $60–$80 an hour with an $80 service charge, so expect a minimum charge even for a small job.

Most Sacramento handymen do not price a 20-minute fix at 20 minutes of pay. The hourly range, a service charge, or a minimum fee covers the drive, the tools, and the first hour — which is why bundling a short list into one visit is the main way to pay less per job.

What changes the price in Sacramento?

The list, not just the hour. One visit that covers a sticking door in Land Park, a drywall patch in Midtown, and shelves in the same home spreads one service charge across three jobs. Three separate visits pay that charge three times.

Where you live in the metro. The Sacramento metro is wide. A handyman based downtown covering Antelope (95843), Roseville and Rocklin (95678–95765), and Lincoln (95648) is driving real miles between jobs, and outlying jobs are more likely to carry a minimum or a travel charge. Jobs inside the central ZIPs — Downtown (95814), Midtown (95816), and Land Park (95818) — sit inside most service areas.

Skill and licensing. Basic repairs and installs sit at the low end of the range. Work that needs a trade license or a specialized skill — electrical or plumbing work, for example — prices higher, and some of it legally needs a licensed trade, not a handyman (HomeBlue).

Emergency and timing. An urgent, same-day call, work that spans several days, or a job that needs a permit or special equipment all push the price up. Flexible scheduling and a job that finishes in one day push it down (HomeBlue).

How to pay less without getting worse work

  • Bundle the list. Write down every small repair first, then book one visit for all of it — this is the single biggest saving on handyman work.
  • Supply your own materials. Buy the faucet, fan, or fixture yourself when you can, and avoid the 10–30% materials markup.
  • Get the price shape in writing. Ask whether you are paying hourly, flat per job, or hourly plus a service charge — and what the minimum is — before the visit starts.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a handyman charge per hour in Sacramento?

$50 to $100 an hour on average (HomeBlue). Sacramento listings on HomeAdvisor show general rates of $60–$80 an hour.

How much is a handyman for a half day or a full day in Sacramento?

About $210 to $400 for 4 hours and $420 to $790 for 8 hours, plus materials (HomeBlue).

Why is there a service charge or minimum for a small job?

It covers travel, tools, and the first hour. HomeAdvisor’s Sacramento listings show a service charge of about $80, and Angi notes most handymen charge a minimum call-out fee for single small jobs.

What jobs should I not give a handyman?

Bigger projects that require licenses and permits — a bathroom remodel, structural work, or new electrical or plumbing runs — need a licensed contractor. A handyman is for repairs, installs, and maintenance in one visit (Angi, HomeBlue).
