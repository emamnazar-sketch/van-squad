How much does a handyman cost in Boulder?

The short answer. Plan on $60 to $120 an hour in Boulder, depending on who you hire and how they bill. BIG WORKS’ modeled Boulder rate — built from the local wage, not a survey — is $60 to $85 an hour. A Front Range operator working in Boulder, Handy Andy, publishes $75 an hour ($65 if you mention its website), with a one-hour minimum. FixUpFirst puts Boulder higher, at $80 to $120 an hour. Boulder has no surveyed citywide average, so get the minimum in writing before anyone starts.

Read the hourly rows by method. The $27.52 figure is what a handyman earns in the Boulder metro, not what you pay. BIG WORKS turns that wage into a $60 to $85 billed rate by adding overhead and profit — its own model, labeled modeled here. Handy Andy’s $75 is a real published price for work in Boulder. FixUpFirst’s $80 to $120 sits above both. Those gaps are normal in Boulder. What matters is the minimum and whether materials are at cost or marked up.

This is where people overpay in Boulder. A 20-minute fix billed at a two-hour minimum costs the same as two hours of work. Handy Andy’s minimum is one hour. Ask every quote the same question: what is the smallest bill I can get? Then stack your small jobs into that visit.

Why does Boulder sit at the top of Colorado’s pay table?

Boulder has the highest median handyman wage among the Colorado areas BIG WORKS lists — $27.52 an hour, ahead of Denver-Aurora at $25.21 and Fort Collins-Loveland at $25.62. Pay is not price, but price follows pay. A shop that has to hire at Boulder wages cannot bill at Colorado Springs rates, where BIG WORKS models $50 to $70 an hour.

That does not make every high Boulder quote fair. It means the honest comparison is Boulder to Boulder: a modeled $60 to $85, a published $75, and a guide’s $80 to $120. A quote well above $120 an hour for plain small repairs needs a reason — a license the job actually requires, weekend work, or a two-person crew. Ask for the reason in writing.

What does coverage in ZIP 80301 actually cover?

Our directory’s Boulder handyman coverage sits in one ZIP. All 8 Boulder listings on Van Squads cover ZIP 80301 — the east side of Boulder, out toward Gunbarrel. Handy Andy’s published service area names Boulder and Gunbarrel too, with no travel fee inside its main area and materials billed at cost, item by item.

That single-ZIP spread matters when you book. If your home is in central or west Boulder, say your exact ZIP in the first message. A business covering 80301 may still come to you, but the slot and any travel charge should be settled before the visit, not on your doorstep.

Which jobs belong to a handyman, and which need a licensed trade?

Small repairs and installs are handyman work: mounting, assembly, drywall patches, caulking, door adjustments, fixture swaps. Work that needs a permit, or that changes plumbing, electrical, or structural systems, belongs to a licensed trade in Colorado. FixUpFirst draws the same line nationally — fixture swaps are fine, rerouting lines or running new wiring is not.

Hiring a handyman for licensed work to save a few dollars an hour is the cheap mistake. The redo, plus a permit problem when you sell, is the expensive one.

How do you keep a small Boulder job small?

Write the list before you call. Five small jobs in one visit use the minimum once. Five separate visits pay it five times.

Get three things in the quote: the rate or flat price, the minimum, and how materials are billed. Handy Andy bills materials at cost, line by line. Hold other quotes to that. If a quote will not split labor and materials, you cannot tell what you are paying for either one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a handyman charge per hour in Boulder?

Most Boulder quotes land between $60 and $120 an hour. BIG WORKS models $60 to $85 from the local wage, Handy Andy publishes $75 an hour for Boulder work, and FixUpFirst puts Boulder at $80 to $120.

What is the minimum charge for a handyman in Boulder?

It depends on the shop. Handy Andy, which works in Boulder and Gunbarrel, has a one-hour minimum at $75 an hour ($65 if you mention its website) and no travel fee in its main area. Nationally, Thumbtack puts typical handyman minimums at $60 to $100. Always ask — the minimum is often the whole bill on a small job.

Why is Boulder more expensive than other Colorado cities?

Boulder’s median handyman wage is $27.52 an hour, the highest among the Colorado areas BIG WORKS lists, and billed rates follow local pay. BIG WORKS models Boulder at $60 to $85 an hour against $50 to $70 for Colorado Springs.

How much does TV mounting cost in Boulder?

BIG WORKS’ Colorado estimate for TV mounting is $100 to $350, labor plus materials, scaled to Colorado wages — it is a state estimate, not a Boulder survey. At a one-hour minimum, a simple mount on drywall should sit near the bottom of that range; brick, a mount you supply late, or wire concealment pushes it up.

Can a Boulder handyman do plumbing or electrical work?

Fixture-level swaps, yes — a faucet, a toilet, a light fixture. Rerouting plumbing, running new wiring, and any work that needs a permit belongs to a licensed trade in Colorado. Ask which side of that line your job sits on before you book.
