How much does mobile car detailing cost in Kansas City, Kansas?

The short answer. A full mobile detail on the Kansas side runs $200 to $250 for a car at Ride and Shine KC, and $250 to $300 for an SUV or truck. SmartShine in Overland Park starts at $275, and Premier’s Johnson County full detail is $350 for a sedan. A September 2026 survey of Kansas City shops put a full detail at $129 to $600 for a sedan, with a $314 median.

This is where people overpay. The same words — "full detail" — cover a $200 job and a $350 job here. The gap is what is in the package: clay work, a sealant that lasts, steam and extraction inside. Compare the task list, not the package name.

What changes the price in Kansas City, Kansas?

Van Squads coverage for this category sits in south Overland Park, and so do most of these prices. Every listing in this metro covers ZIP 66223 — Deer Creek, Lions Gate, and Mills Farm country, in Johnson County. Johnson County lists sit at the high end of the metro: Masters’ Guild puts an Overland Park full detail at $140 to $320, and Premier’s is $350 for a sedan, with no travel fee in the county. A cheaper quote from across the river isn’t a quote for your driveway.

Size adds a flat step, and the step is published. Lighthouse adds $20 for an SUV or truck and $40 for a three-row on its full combo. Premier steps $350 to $400 to $450 across the same sizes. Ride and Shine’s complete detail is $50 more for an SUV or truck than for a car. Quote your actual vehicle — a sedan price won’t hold for a three-row.

November is brine season in Kansas. A Lenexa operator’s guide notes Kansas roads start seeing anti-icing brine in the first week of October, and by Thanksgiving the salt trucks are running. Brine coats the lower 18 to 24 inches of the car. Wyandotte County’s snow page adds this: below 15°F, salt works more slowly, so it sits on your rocker panels longer. A wash and sealant now costs less than correction work in March.

Add-ons are where two quotes drift apart. Pet hair removal is $30 or more at Ride and Shine, $40 at Lighthouse, and a flat $95 at Premier. Engine bay work is $40 at Ride and Shine and $95 at Premier. Headlight restoration is $50 at Ride and Shine and starts at $79 at SmartShine. Name your add-ons first.

Ask who brings the water and power. Ride and Shine says it’s self-contained. Lighthouse needs an outdoor outlet and a spigot at your house. That decides whether a detail can happen in an apartment lot, or only in your driveway.

How long does a mobile detail take in Kansas City, Kansas?

SmartShine puts a job at 1 to 5 hours at your home or office. Premier puts its full detail at 4 to 5 hours. Ride and Shine’s express clean takes 45 to 60 minutes. Plan on the long end for a first detail after a long gap.

How to pay less and still get a real detail

  • Detail the half that’s dirty. An interior refresh at $120 to $150 fixes the part you sit in. If the inside is fine, a $95 to $125 exterior is the whole job.
  • Get the sealant before the salt. A spray sealant is a $50 add-on at Ride and Shine. Correction after a winter of brine costs far more.
  • Name the extras on the phone. Pet hair, an engine bay, a cloudy headlight — priced up front, they’re line items. Found on the driveway, they’re surprises.
  • Keep it up instead of rescuing it. An express clean at $50 to $85 between full details keeps the car in the cheap band.
