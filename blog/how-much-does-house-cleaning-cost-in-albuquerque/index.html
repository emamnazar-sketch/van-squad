How much does house cleaning cost in Albuquerque?

The short answer. In Albuquerque, house cleaning starts at about $20.53 an hour on Care.com, up to $24.63 — a bit under the national starting average of $21.61. An independent maid averages about $15 an hour locally (Care.com). A company crew costs more: one published Albuquerque list charges $58.85 per labor hour, with a regular clean starting at $184.50 for 700 sq ft or less, $235.40 for 701–2,000 sq ft, and $294.25 for over 2,000 sq ft (Maid O’Matic).

This is where people overpay — or think they are. A $15-an-hour independent and a $58.85 company rate are not the same product. The company rate is per labor hour: two cleaners for two hours is four labor hours. The crew brings supplies and insurance and finishes faster. Compare the total for your home, not the hourly number alone.

Rio Rancho is a useful check. Care.com puts its starting rate at $24.28 an hour — higher than Albuquerque’s $20.53. Same metro, different price, so a quote from across the river is no benchmark for a Northeast Heights home.

What changes the price in Albuquerque?

Home size, in square feet. The local list prices by size first: $184.50 to $235.40 past 700 sq ft, $294.25 past 2,000 sq ft. Larger homes run about 3 labor hours per 1,000 sq ft. Bathrooms add time on top of floor space.

Dust and condition. Fine desert dust settles on tile, blinds, and baseboards here. A home gone months without a real clean is priced as a first clean, not a regular visit — on the local list, that starts at $500.23, well above the $184.50–$294.25 regular starts. That gap is buildup, not a penalty.

How far you are from the crew. Maid O’Matic bases its pricing on homes within 15 driving miles of its office at 2901 Juan Tabo Blvd NE, 87112. Past 15 miles it offers deep and move cleans only, each starting at $500.23 whatever the size. A home in Rio Rancho (87124, 87144) or on the Westside (87120, 87121) can price differently from the same home in 87109 — ask if travel is in the quote.

How to pay less without getting a thin clean

  • Go recurring after the first clean. On the local list, weekly takes 15% off, every-two-week 10% off. Visits are shorter too — the home never builds back up.
  • Do the first clean once, properly. One deep reset held with biweekly visits beats paying deep prices again after a long gap.
  • Clear surfaces before they arrive. You pay for labor hours. Time moving mail and toys is time not spent cleaning.
  • Ask what a labor hour means on your quote. How many cleaners, how many hours, what minimum? That answer explains most of the spread between quotes.
