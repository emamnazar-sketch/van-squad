How much does mobile car detailing cost in Phoenix?

The short answer. Most Phoenix mobile detailers charge about $150 to $350 for a full interior and exterior detail on a car, with a full detail at roughly $175 to $350 and a typical figure around $260. SUVs and trucks run higher, commonly $220 to $500. A wash and wax costs less, $65 to $120, and a platform list in Phoenix starts an express detail at $109 — a lighter package, priced as one.

This is where people overpay. Washos starts at $109 and Optic’s Scottsdale full detail is $209 for a car, while the Phoenix guide’s typical full detail sits around $260. Those aren’t the same visit. The low lists are a wash, wax, vacuum, and wipe-down. The higher ones add extraction, clay, and sealant. Compare what’s in the package, not the number on top.

Why does desert driving change the price in Phoenix?

Dust here is grit, not dirt. Fine desert dust acts like a micro-abrasive on paint, and a proper wash has to lift it off before a mitt touches the car. After a haboob, that dust is in the carpets and door jambs too, and Impeccable’s Phoenix guide names monsoon sand and dust as extra time on the job.

Sun damage is its own line item. Phoenix averages roughly 299 sunny days a year, and UV breaks down clear coat, fades trim, and cooks headlights cloudy. Faded trim and oxidized headlights are add-on work, not part of a basic detail, on the Phoenix guide. A wax top-up every two to three months in summer is Washos’ advice for cars parked outside in Biltmore or Desert Ridge.

Hard water spots bake on fast. Phoenix tap water is hard, and sprinkler overspray leaves chalky mineral spots that set in the heat. Spots baked on by sprinklers and sun may need a dedicated remover or polishing, per the Phoenix guide.

How much more for an SUV or truck in Scottsdale?

Size is the cleanest price jump in the Valley. On the Phoenix guide, a mobile full detail runs about $160 to $400 for a sedan or coupe and $220 to $500 for an SUV or pickup. Optic’s Scottsdale list keeps the gap flat: $209 for a car, $229 for an SUV or pickup.

Washos splits it by add-on. Its Phoenix prices are for a sedan, and a mid-size SUV or crossover adds $20, a full-size SUV, minivan, or truck adds $40, and a commercial vehicle adds $50. A third row means more carpet, seats, and glass. If a quote jumps by hundreds over size alone, ask what else got added.

What about ceramic coating in Phoenix?

Ceramic is priced apart from a detail, every time. Washos lists its Diamond Ceramic coating in Phoenix from $499 for a sedan, with a one-step polish before the coating goes on. The Phoenix guide puts paint correction on its own at $345 to $700, which is the work that has to happen before any coating is worth paying for.

In Phoenix the case for coating is the sun, not the shine. Clear coat fades faster here than almost anywhere in the country, and regulars book ceramic refreshes ahead of summer for that reason, per Washos. On a car you plan to keep and park outside in 85251, it can pay. On a car you’ll sell next year, a $209 to $269 Scottsdale full or premium detail with a sealant is the honest spend.

How to pay less and still get a real detail

  • Buy the half that is dirty. Interior only at $149 to $250 in the Valley beats a full detail when the outside is fine.
  • Name the extras before you book. Pet hair, shampoo, odor work, and engine bay cleaning each add time and cost — get them in the quote.
  • Ask if mobile costs extra. The Phoenix guide says mobile usually runs about the same as a shop. Some charge a travel fee, so ask.
  • Keep a schedule. A full detail every three to six months suits most daily drivers here, with plain washes between.

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Phoenix?

Most mobile detailers charge about $150 to $350 for a full detail on a car, roughly $175 to $350 with a typical figure around $260 on the Phoenix guide. Optic’s Scottsdale full detail is $209 for a car and $229 for an SUV or pickup, and Washos starts an express detail at $109.

Do mobile detailers charge more than shops in Phoenix?

Usually not much. The Phoenix guide says published mobile prices are close to shop prices. Some detailers add a travel fee for longer distances.

How often should I detail my car in Arizona?

A full detail every three to six months suits most daily drivers, with regular washes between, per the Phoenix guide. Washos suggests a wax top-up every two to three months in summer.

Can a detail protect my car from Phoenix sun?

A wax or sealant helps for weeks to months, and a ceramic coating lasts longer. Washos says Phoenix sun fades clear coat faster than almost anywhere in the US, and its Phoenix ceramic coating starts at $499 for a sedan.

Do mobile detailers handle monsoon dust and haboob residue in Phoenix?

Yes, and it takes longer. Washos says haboobs leave a fine dust film that bonds quickly when wet, lifted with a foam pre-soak and a clay bar when buildup is heavy.
