How much does mobile car detailing cost in Omaha?

The short answer. In Omaha, a full mobile detail runs $275 for a sedan and $339–$399 for an SUV or truck on Flawless Mobile Car Detailing's list, and $325–$550 for an inside-and-out package at Invictus. Exterior-only starts at $99, interior-only at $170. The car never leaves your driveway.

Read the package, not the headline. K3's $200 sedan bundle and ANKA's $499 complete appointment are both "full details" on paper. They are not the same hours of work.

This is where people overpay: ceramic and flagship money on a daily driver. Invictus puts its Diamond Detail at $1,500–$2,400, and Prima's paint correction runs to $1,550 for a sedan. On the family SUV that eats crackers in the back seat, a $275–$399 full detail does the job.

What changes the price in Omaha?

Vehicle size, every time. Prima steps a full detail from $275 for a sedan to $355 for a 3-row SUV. Flawless steps it from $275 to $399 for a truck or van. More rows, more glass, more carpet — more time.

Pet hair is priced all over the map here. Flawless charges $45 for heavy pet hair removal. Prima starts at $100 and goes up with severity, assessed on arrival. Hometown charges $139.99. Same dog, three prices. Send a photo of the back seat before you book and get the number in writing.

November means salt season is coming. Omaha winters drop road salt and brine on everything. ANKA runs a Salt & Brine Reset from $289, a 2–3 hour winter recovery detail. Flawless built its exterior pitch around pulling road salt and bug splatter off the paint — the bug part is the I-80 summer drive, the salt part starts now. A detail before the first brine trucks roll is cheaper than correction work in March.

Cold changes how mobile works. Prima's rule: below 32°F, service defaults to drop-off unless you can provide a heated garage. At 32°F or higher, mobile runs as usual. A January booking may not happen in your driveway at all.

Water and power — ask who brings them. Prima and Invictus both run self-contained units, with their own water and power. K3 says the opposite: it has to use your power and water, and mobile is free within 25 miles of Omaha, then $1 a mile. In a downtown (68102) apartment lot with no spigot, that decides who can take the job.

How to pay less without a worse detail

  • Buy the half you need. If the paint is fine and the cabin is the problem, interior-only runs $170–$175 for a sedan.
  • Use maintenance pricing. Prima's monthly plan starts at $175 a month, and its bi-weekly sedan detail is $85. A car on a plan never needs the big rescue detail.
  • Name the mess up front. Pet hair, stains, and odor are add-ons on every Omaha list — $35 for stain extraction and $59 for odor treatment at Flawless. Priced before the visit, they're line items. Found during it, they're a renegotiation.
  • Get the total before work starts. Flawless lists upfront pricing and Invictus posts its rates before you book. If a quote can't be written down, keep looking.

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Omaha?

$275 for a sedan on two Omaha lists (Prima, Flawless), up to $399 for a truck or van at Flawless and $325–$550 at Invictus. Exterior-only starts at $99, interior-only at $170.

How long does a full detail take in Omaha?

Flawless puts an interior or full detail at 2 to 3 hours and an exterior at about an hour. Prima puts a full detail at 3 to 5 hours, and ANKA's complete appointment runs 4 to 6 hours.

How much does interior-only detailing cost in Omaha?

$170 for a sedan at Prima and $175 at Flawless. Invictus puts interior packages at $225–$400. Seat or carpet shampoo adds $75 each at Prima; heavy pet hair adds $45 at Flawless and starts at $100 at Prima.

Do mobile detailers in Omaha bring their own water and power?

Some do. Prima and Invictus both run self-contained units. K3 uses your power and water. Ask before you book, especially in an apartment lot or office garage.

Can you get a mobile detail in Omaha in winter?

Yes, with a limit. Prima defaults to drop-off below 32°F unless you have a heated garage; mobile runs as usual at 32°F or higher. Salt and brine are the winter job here — ANKA's Salt & Brine Reset starts at $289.
