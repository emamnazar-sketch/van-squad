How much does house cleaning cost in Santa Fe?

The short answer. A routine house cleaning in Santa Fe runs about $115 to $190 a visit, with a modeled typical price of $150, per green sweep’s 2026 New Mexico pricing guide. Biweekly cleaning for a 2,000 sq ft home runs $140 to $210 a visit. MaidProfit puts the New Mexico standard clean at $95 to $210, averaging $140 — and Santa Fe sits at the top of the state market, so expect the upper half of that range here. Ask whether your quote includes Santa Fe’s 8.4375% gross receipts tax.

Two honest warnings. First, there is no surveyed Santa Fe average. Green sweep says that on its own page: the Santa Fe figures are published estimates, not a measured citywide average. Second, the models disagree because they measure different things. Care.com’s $19.59 is what individual cleaners in New Mexico post as a starting rate. BIG WORKS’ $40 to $55 is a modeled company billed rate for Santa Fe, built from the local cleaner’s wage. A company quote and an individual’s rate will never match, and neither is wrong.

This is where people overpay in Santa Fe. They compare a company’s flat quote against an individual’s hourly post and book the “cheaper” one without asking what is included — supplies, insurance, a second cleaner, and the tax. Get the full visit price in writing, with the tax line showing.

Why does Santa Fe cost more than the rest of New Mexico?

Santa Fe is the state’s premium cleaning market, and the sources say why. MaidProfit points to the tourism-driven economy and higher-end homes: vacation rentals and second homes need cleaning on a host’s schedule, not a homeowner’s, and that work pays more. Its New Mexico average clean is $142, a bit below the national $157 — Santa Fe pulls the state figure up, not down.

The New Mexico deep-cleaning guide puts numbers on the gap: Santa Fe prices run 15 to 30 percent above Albuquerque. A deep clean on a 2,000 sq ft Albuquerque home runs $250 to $500, and historic eastside properties with heavy adobe detailing take longer per square foot than a newer home on the south side. More time per room is more money per visit. That is the whole story.

What does adobe and dust do to the quote?

Santa Fe homes collect fine dust in a way a carpeted suburban home does not. TheCleanestRoom’s New Mexico guide names dust as a constant factor here, and the deep-cleaning guide builds its Santa Fe pricing around adobe construction — saltillo tile, vigas, and detailed trim all slow a cleaner down and need gentler handling than vinyl and drywall.

Practical version: a first clean in an adobe home is rarely the cheap visit. Dust sits on beam ends and tile grout, and a crew that knows these houses will price that in. Nearly every New Mexico company sets a minimum of $140 to $175 a visit, per the deep-cleaning guide, so a small home does not scale down the way the square footage suggests. If a quote for a first deep clean in 87501 comes in under that minimum, ask what it leaves out.

Why is there no firm weekly price for Santa Fe?

Does the quote include tax in Santa Fe?
