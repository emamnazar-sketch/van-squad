How much does mobile car detailing cost in Salt Lake City?

The short answer. In Salt Lake City, a full mobile detail starts at $199 at PureCare, $230 for a sedan at Concours, and $299 at Platinum. The Livery Club charges a flat $300 for interior and exterior together. Interior-only starts lower — $149 at PureCare, $180 for a sedan at Concours. Exterior-only starts at $69 at PureCare. Pet hair is an add-on almost everywhere: from $35 at PureCare, from $40 at Summit Precision.

This is where people overpay. A $199 full detail and a $499 Platinum detail aren't the same job at two prices. The $499 tier adds ceramic coating and paint correction on top of the $299 Standard clean. If your paint looks fine and the cabin is the mess, buy the cabin work.

What changes the price in Salt Lake City?

Winter brine stays on the car. Utah's road crews put down salt brine before a storm and magnesium chloride when it gets colder. That mix holds moisture against the metal and builds up in the wheel wells and under the car, so a wash that actually gets those spots matters more here than a quick rinse. Trey's, in Bluffdale, makes the same point: a touchless rinse between details in salt season makes sense. Clean Mobile Detailing suggests a detail every 2 to 3 months in Utah because dust, sun, and road grime do real damage over time. A car that runs to Draper (84020) and back on I-15 all winter carries that film the whole way.

Sun and hard water leave their own marks. PureCare prices two fixes by name: oxidation polishing for sun-oxidized panels, by quote starting at $70, and water spot and mineral removal at $79. Summit Precision treats the dash, console, and door panels with UV protection in its interior work, and Trey's does the same. If your car parks outside in Salt Lake City (84115) all summer, ask whether protection is in the package. It's not in every one.

Size first, then condition. Summit Precision's ladder is plain: $245, $265, $285, $305 for an interior as the vehicle gets bigger, and $165 to $225 for an exterior. Platinum says its figures are starting rates for sedans and compact cars, with larger vehicles and very dirty cars costing more. Same wash, different vehicle, different price.

Pet hair and car seats are priced apart. PureCare lists pet hair removal from $35, Summit Precision from $40. A child seat is its own line too — $45 at The Livery Club, $50 at Trey's. Name both before you book, in Salt Lake City or in Draper, and the quote holds.

Who brings water and power decides where the job can happen. PureCare says its rig is fully self-contained, with onboard water and generator power. Concours brings water, supplies, and equipment, and needs only access to the car and a place to park. Platinum and Clean Mobile Detailing also bring water and power. Trey's goes the other way: you provide power and water. In an apartment lot in 84115, that difference decides whether the booking works at all.

How to pay less without a worse detail

  • Buy the half that's dirty. Interior-only starts at $149–$200, exterior-only at $69–$120. A full detail is the wrong buy when one side is fine.
  • Name the pet hair and the car seat up front. Pet hair runs $35–$40 as an add-on, a child seat $45–$50. Surprises are how a $230 detail becomes a $300 one.
  • Wash the salt off early. Getting brine off in the season is cleaning work. Leave it on till spring and you're paying for correction instead.
  • Ask about water and power before you book. Tell the detailer what your driveway or lot has.

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Salt Lake City?

A full mobile detail starts at $199 at PureCare, $230 for a sedan at Concours, and $299 at Platinum. The Livery Club charges a flat $300 for interior and exterior together. Nationally, mobile detailing averages $144–$254 (Thumbtack, national).

How much does interior-only detailing cost in Salt Lake City?

PureCare starts interior work at $149. Concours starts at $180 for a sedan, The Livery Club charges $200 flat, and Clean Mobile Detailing starts at $197 for a sedan. Summit Precision charges $245 for a standard-size vehicle, up to $305 for an oversized one.

How much does pet hair removal add in Salt Lake City?

PureCare lists pet hair removal from $35, for hair embedded in carpets, seats, and seams. Summit Precision lists it from $40. Both price it apart from the base package, so ask before you book.

How long does a mobile detail take in Salt Lake City?

Concours puts a sedan full detail at about 3 hours, and SUVs and trucks at 3.5 to 4 hours. Clean Mobile Detailing puts most details at 2 to 4 hours. PureCare puts a standard interior at 1.5 to 2.5 hours and a premium interior at 2.5 to 4 hours.

Do I need to provide water or electricity in Salt Lake City?

Often, no. PureCare, Concours, Platinum, and Clean Mobile Detailing say they bring their own water and power. Trey's asks you to provide power and water. Check which rule your detailer follows before you book an apartment or office lot.
