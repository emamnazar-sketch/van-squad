How much does house cleaning cost in Tulsa?

The short answer. In Tulsa, a standard house cleaning runs $95 to $210 on MaidProfit’s Tulsa row, at $20 to $34 an hour per cleaner. Care.com puts an individual Tulsa maid at about $17.40 an hour — solo cleaners, not companies. Local companies price by the visit: South Tulsa Cleaning Co. charges $99 to $139 for every-other-week service by room count, and EL Fresh Clean starts a 3-bedroom home at $158 weekly, $179 every two weeks, and $210 monthly.

This is where people overpay. A $99 every-other-week clean and a $350 deep clean aren’t the same job at two prices. The $99 visit in South Tulsa (74133) is a standard package on a home that stays tidy — dusting, bathrooms, kitchen, floors. The deep clean adds baseboards, inside the oven, cabinet work, and behind furniture. If your home gets cleaned every two weeks, you need the $99 kind of price, not the $350 one.

What changes the price in Tulsa?

Wind puts dust back inside, fast. Squeegee Squad’s Tulsa guide puts it plainly: Oklahoma ranks among the windiest states, and strong winds carry dust across the state, with spring pollen and summer storms adding more. That film lands on sills, fans, and blinds in a Midtown or Brookside home just as it lands on glass. If dusting only covers flat surfaces, a Tulsa home looks dusty again in days. Ask whether fans, blinds, and sills are in the visit before you book.

A lot of Tulsa homes are older, and older homes take longer. Masters’ Guild’s Tulsa page, using Census-derived figures, notes that 53% of owner-occupied homes and 50% of renter-occupied homes in Tulsa were built from 1950 to 1979. Homes in Kendall Whittier, Maple Ridge, and Cherry Street from that era often have more trim, older tubs, and grout that needs scrubbing, not a quick wipe. Name the age and condition of your home when you ask for a quote — a surprise there reprices the visit.

How often you book is the discount. EL Fresh Clean prices a 3-bedroom home at $158 a visit weekly, $179 every two weeks, and $210 monthly. South Tulsa Cleaning Co. builds its whole list around every-other-week visits, at $99, $119, and $139 by room count — and notes weekly runs lower, every four weeks higher. MaidProfit’s Oklahoma ranges agree: weekly $75 to $170 a visit against $100 to $220 monthly.

Deep and move-out work sits in its own tier. EL Fresh Clean starts deep cleaning at $350 and move-out cleaning at $295 for a 3-bedroom home, against $158 for a weekly standard visit. MaidProfit’s Oklahoma ranges put deep cleans at $180 to $380 and move-outs at $200 to $420. Don’t budget a move-out in Tulsa (74137) from the standard-clean price — an empty home gets checked room by room, and the quote reflects that.

How to pay less without a worse clean

  • Go recurring, even monthly. Every Tulsa list checked prices repeat visits under one-time work for the same home.
  • Count rooms and bathrooms honestly. South Tulsa Cleaning Co. prices by rooms and baths — $99, $119, or $139. A wrong count moves you a tier at the door.
  • Match the tier to the mess. A tidy home on an every-other-week plan doesn’t need a $350 deep clean each visit. Save that for a first visit or a seasonal reset.
  • Say so if only part of the home needs cleaning. South Tulsa Cleaning Co. will price just the lived-in areas of a larger home and price the rest separately when you ask.
