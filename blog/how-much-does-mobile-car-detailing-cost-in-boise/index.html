How much does mobile car detailing cost in Boise?

The short answer. In Boise, most complete details run $250 to $450 for a sedan and $350 to $600 for a truck or SUV (Boise Mobile Auto Detailing's price guide). A basic inside-and-out package starts at $195 (Summit Prestige). Interior-only work runs $175 to $400. A full detail takes 3 to 5 hours at your home.

The spread is real. Gatlin's Idaho guide puts a sedan full detail at $140 to $220. Gemstar's standard full detail starts at $319. Those are not the same job — check what is in the package, not just the headline price.

This is where people overpay: paying Platinum money, $1,499 and up at Gemstar, for a daily driver that needs a standard clean. That top package is paint correction, ceramic coating and a full interior restoration. Most Boise cars do not need it.

What changes the price in Boise?

Vehicle size, first. Every Boise list prices it in. Peak Precision adds $50 for a large or three-row vehicle on every package — its standard inside-and-out job goes from $330 to $380. Summit Prestige counts any three-row vehicle, and any half-ton truck or bigger, as large.

What Boise roads and sun do to a car. Gemstar's Boise exterior page lists the local wear plainly: road grime from I-84, bug splatter on summer highway drives, road salt through the winter, and UV from Boise's 200-plus sunny days. A car parked outside in West Boise (83713) all summer needs paint protection a garaged car does not. That is the honest reason an exterior quote climbs.

Pet hair, kids and condition. RPC in Boise prices pet hair removal at $25 to $50 on top of a full interior detail, an engine bay at $50 to $75, and a clay bar at $50. Name the mess before you book. A quote given sight unseen is a guess.

Water and power at your spot — ask. Boise operators split on this. Boise Mobile Auto Detailing, Gemstar and Summit Prestige say their rigs carry their own. Peak Precision says the opposite: it needs a working water supply at your place. In an apartment lot in West Boise with no spigot, that answer decides who can do the job at all.

How to pay less without a worse detail

  • Use maintenance pricing if you qualify. Peak Precision's four-week maintenance inside-and-out package is $220, against $330 standard — but your car has to have been detailed in the previous four weeks.
  • Book the interior only if the paint is fine. Interior-only work runs $135 to $219 at the basic and standard levels (Summit Prestige, Gemstar). No need to pay full-detail money for a cabin problem.
  • Do two cars at once. Summit Prestige's two-vehicle interior special is $150 per car for its Prestige Interior, which lists at $255 for one.
  • Get the quote confirmed before work starts. Boise Mobile Auto Detailing's guide says every quote is confirmed before work begins, and travel fees can apply outside its core area. Ask up front if you are out toward Nampa or Caldwell.
