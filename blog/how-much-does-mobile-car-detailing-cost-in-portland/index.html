How much does mobile car detailing cost in Portland?

The short answer. In Portland, a full mobile detail runs $250 for a sedan, $300 for an SUV or truck, and $350 for a large SUV at Bridge City Detailing — one flat rate by size. Royal Detailing PDX lists a full detail at $240. Mad Dog starts at $480 for a car, but that one includes a one-step paint correction and paint protection — a different job. Interior-only work runs $150–$250 at Bridge City and $180 at Royal.

This is where people overpay. A $240 full detail and a $480 full detail aren’t two prices for the same work. Mad Dog’s $480 adds a one-step paint correction and paint protection to the inside-and-out clean. If your paint is fine and the cabin is the mess, that’s correction you never asked for. What changes the price in Portland?

Rain, sap, and moss set the Portland list. Armor Auto Detailing, a Portland-area mobile operator, puts it plainly: rain, road spray, tree sap, and mineral deposits leave paint dull even after a wash — and that’s before moss and pollen. Ace Cars in Tigard sets its final price by the same stuff — pet hair, water spots, tree sap, road tar, and paint oxidation are all named factors. Sap isn’t a quick wipe. It adds time, and time is the price.

Size first, then condition. Bridge City charges $250, $300, or $350 by size alone, and says heavily soiled interiors and extreme pet hair may run higher, confirmed before work starts. Mad Dog prices pet hair removal as an add-on at $40 an hour and odor removal at $100. A clean sedan and a Tigard SUV that hauls a wet dog all winter aren’t the same job at any shop in town.

Mobile in the rain is a real question here. Bridge City says interior details are generally possible rain or shine, though cover helps, and exterior work gets rescheduled if the forecast turns into a real downpour. Ask before you book a November appointment, not the morning of.

Who brings water and power changes who can take the job. Bridge City needs a power outlet and, for exterior work, a hose at your place — interior-only can run without one. Royal says you need to provide nothing. In a Tigard apartment lot (97223), that difference decides the booking.

Protection before the wet months pays back. Armor notes protection added after a clean helps grime release faster in the wet months — the car stays cleaner, and the next wash is cheaper work. A sealant added to a Tigard complete detail runs $80 at Ace Cars.

How to pay less without a worse detail

  • Buy the half that’s dirty. Interior-only runs $150–$250 at Bridge City and $180 at Royal. Exterior-only is $140 at Royal. A full detail is the wrong buy when one side is fine.
  • Describe the car honestly before you book. Ace Cars gives an approximate price from a good description — age, dirt, pet hair, stains, sap — and sets the final price at the appointment. Photos do the same job.
  • Skip the correction if the paint doesn’t need it. A flat full detail at $240–$350 covers the clean. Correction and ceramic work start at $480 and $800 at Mad Dog — worth it on a car you keep, hard to justify on one you’re selling soon.
  • Ask about water and power up front. Tell the detailer what your driveway or lot has. Surprises on the day cost more than the question.

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Portland?

A full mobile detail runs $250 for a sedan, $300 for an SUV or truck, and $350 for a large SUV at Bridge City Detailing. Royal Detailing PDX lists a full detail at $240, and Mad Dog starts at $480 for a car with paint correction included. Nationally, mobile detailing averages $144–$254 (Thumbtack, national).

How much does interior-only detailing cost in Portland?

Bridge City charges $150–$250 by size and condition, and Royal lists an interior detail at $180. Mad Dog starts higher at $250 for a car, $275 for an SUV or truck, and $325 for a large SUV or truck.

Why does one Portland full detail cost $240 and another $480?

The packages cover different work. Royal’s $240 is a complete interior and exterior detail. Mad Dog’s $480 adds a one-step paint correction and paint protection. Ask what’s included, in writing, before you pick on price alone.

Can a mobile detailer detail my car in the rain in Portland?

Often, yes for the inside. Bridge City says interior details are generally possible rain or shine, with cover preferred, and exterior work is rescheduled if a real downpour is coming. Ask your detailer what their rain rule is when you book.
