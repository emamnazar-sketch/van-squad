How much does house cleaning cost in Grand Forks?

The short answer. A standard house cleaning in Grand Forks runs $90 to $190, averaging $130 a visit on MaidProfit’s Grand Forks page. Deep cleaning averages $245 ($160 to $350) and a move-out clean averages $285 ($190 to $400) on the same page. MaidProfit puts Grand Forks rates 14% below the national average — the lowest in North Dakota on its state page — and a weekly recurring visit drops to $80 to $160, averaging $115.

This is where people overpay in Grand Forks. They pay a first-visit deep price on every visit. On a weekly or every-two-weeks rhythm, you should be on the standard or recurring rate. If visit four still costs like visit one, ask what is still deep about it.

Why do UND and the Air Force Base set the cleaning calendar here?

Grand Forks runs on two calendars that are not yours. MaidProfit’s Grand Forks page says the city is defined by the University of North Dakota and Grand Forks Air Force Base: UND’s 14,000 students and 3,000 employees drive August move-ins, December deep cleans, and May move-outs, and the base brings frequent PCS moves that need move-in and move-out cleans.

That bunching is real money if you are in it. A campus-area move-out runs $150 to $300 per unit on MaidProfit, below its $190 to $400 general move-out band, because a student unit is smaller than a family house. But the cheap unit price does you no good if every cleaner in town is booked the last week of May. If your lease or your orders give you a date, book the clean when you get the date.

Renting near campus or the base? Get the landlord’s checklist in the quote, in writing. Deposit money turns on ovens, window tracks, and walls, not on whether the living room looks fine.

What does a Grand Forks winter add to the bill?

CleanSpace’s Grand Forks guide is blunt about the local work: winters drag salt and slush into entryways, calcium chloride and rock salt end up on carpets and hardwood, and white salt stains can ruin a floor finish if they sit. Its fall note is the other half of the year — harvest dust off the Red River Valley farmland settles over the city, into window tracks and on exterior doors.

So an entry floor in Riverside or the South End is not a two-minute wipe in February. Tell the cleaner if your entry takes the full hit from the street. That time is real.

Older houses near downtown and the Near Southside add a different cost. CleanSpace names the historic homes there — original hardwood, crown molding, and radiators that collect dust between the columns. That work is slower and gentler, not the same pass as a newer South End house.

How much do deep cleans and move-outs cost?

Use the Grand Forks rows. Deep cleaning is $160 to $350, averaging $245 on MaidProfit. HomeStars puts deep cleaning at $30 to $60 an hour in Grand Forks, above its $25 to $50 general rate, which lands in the same place for a half-day deep clean.

Move-out cleaning is $190 to $400, averaging $285 on MaidProfit, with campus-area units at $150 to $300. A small apartment with light wear should sit near the bottom. A family house that needs the oven, the fridge, and every wall wiped will sit near the top, and it should — get that list in the quote before anyone starts.

What does coverage in ZIPs 58201, 58202, 58203, and 56721 actually cover?

Our directory’s Grand Forks house cleaning coverage sits in four ZIPs. ZIPs 58201, 58202, and 58203 cover Grand Forks itself — downtown and the Near Southside, the University District and University Village, Riverside along the river, Lincoln Park, the Columbia Mall area, and the South End. All 6 Grand Forks listings on Van Squads cover those three ZIPs. ZIP 56721 is East Grand Forks, across the Red River in Minnesota, covered by the featured business below and one other listing in this cell.

That river crossing matters when you book. Say which side of the river you are on in the first message, not after the quote.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a standard house cleaning in Grand Forks?

$90 to $190, averaging $130 a visit on MaidProfit’s Grand Forks page. Its North Dakota page puts a Grand Forks standard clean at $100 to $220, with cleaners at $22 to $37 an hour per cleaner.

Why is Grand Forks the cheapest city in North Dakota for cleaning?

MaidProfit says so plainly on both pages: Grand Forks has the lowest rates in the state, 14% below the national average on its city page, and it ties that to the city’s lower cost of living as a college town, below the oil-producing western cities.

How much does a move-out clean cost near UND?

Campus-area move-outs are priced per unit at $150 to $300 on MaidProfit’s Grand Forks page. A general move-out in Grand Forks runs $190 to $400, averaging $285. Book May and August dates early — those are the turnover months MaidProfit names.

Does recurring cleaning actually save money in Grand Forks?

Yes, on the published list. MaidProfit puts a weekly recurring visit at $80 to $160, averaging $115, against a $130 standard average. The first visit usually costs more, because it is often priced as a deep clean to bring the house up to a maintainable level.

Why is an individual housekeeper cheaper than a company in Grand Forks?

Care.com’s Grand Forks housekeepers post an average of $17.00 an hour ($17.33 in its FAQ) — that is one person, usually with your supplies. A company rate covers a crew, supplies, insurance, and the office behind them: HomeStars puts general cleaning at $25 to $50 an hour in Grand Forks, and BIG WORKS’ modeled company billed rate is $40 to $55 an hour.
