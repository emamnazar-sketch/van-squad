How much does pool cleaning cost in Albuquerque?

The short answer. Pool cleaning in Albuquerque runs around $29 a visit, with a range of $27–$31 a visit based on pool size and type (HomeBlue’s Albuquerque page). Weekly service — four visits a month — comes to $107–$125 a month on that same page. Budget $1,600–$1,900 a year all in for cleaning, maintenance, and opening and closing the pool.

What changes the price in Albuquerque?

Wind and dust. Spring winds lift fine dust off the West Mesa and the dry arroyos and drop it straight into your pool. It settles on the floor, fills the skimmer basket, and makes the filter work harder. A windy week in April can do more to a northwest-side pool in Ventana Ranch or Paradise Hills than a calm month in winter.

Sun, evaporation, and hard water. Albuquerque sits a mile up. The sun is strong, chlorine burns off fast in summer, and dry air pulls water out — so you top it off more often. The fill water here is hard, and every top-off adds calcium. That is the white scale on your tile. Keeping chemistry balanced in that cycle is the real job.

Your pool’s setup. HomeBlue lists a large pool, difficult access, trees dropping debris, and a sandy location as reasons a quote lands higher, and a small or salt water pool as reasons it lands lower. A salt system changes the chemical bill, not the need for brushing and vacuuming.

How to pay less without a green pool

  • Stay weekly in summer. Cutting visits in July is how a $29 visit turns into a one-time rescue. Nationally a first-time or one-time cleaning runs $150–$413 — roughly five to ten regular visits.
  • Empty the baskets between visits. After a windy day, a packed skimmer basket blocks circulation and strains the pump. Two minutes with the basket saves the filter and the pump.
  • Cover the pool when a dust day is coming. A cover keeps grit out, slows evaporation, and holds chlorine longer.
  • Plan opening and closing, don’t drift into them. HomeBlue’s $1,600–$1,900 annual figure already includes opening and closing. Nationally those run $300–$550 each, so a pool left to sit all winter usually costs more to bring back.

How much does pool cleaning cost in Albuquerque?

Are chemicals included in the price?

Ask before you book. A standard Albuquerque cleaning package typically includes measuring chemistry levels and adding chemicals, along with vacuuming, cleaning accessories, checking or backwashing the filter, and checking fixtures (HomeBlue). Some quotes bill chemicals on top of the visit price, which is why two quotes for the same pool can look far apart.

Does my pool need weekly service in Albuquerque?

In summer, yes for most pools. Strong sun at this elevation burns off chlorine fast, and spring and summer wind keeps adding dust. Every-other-week can work in the cooler months for a covered or lightly used pool, but a 14-day gap in July is how a clear pool turns cloudy.
