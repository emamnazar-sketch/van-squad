How much does mobile car detailing cost in Seattle?

The short answer. In Seattle, a mobile full detail for a sedan runs $249 at Kim Detailing Co., $285 at Royal Mobile Detailing, and $319 for Victor’s premium full detail. A Seattle guide puts a full detail at $200 to $350 for a sedan and $300 to $450 for a larger SUV or truck. Interior-only and exterior-only work starts lower, around $145 to $165. Size and condition move the quote more than the package name does, so send photos before you book.

The lists split for a plain reason. Kim’s $249 full detail and Suds’ $499.99 Elite detail aren’t the same job. The higher lists add extraction, decontamination, and correction-type work. Ask what’s in the package, in writing, before you compare two numbers.

What pushes a Seattle quote up or down?

Vehicle size is the first lever. On Kim’s list the same full detail goes $249 for a sedan, $279 for an SUV or crossover, $299 for a truck or large SUV, and $329 for a van or minivan. Premium runs $449 to $549 on the same steps. Quote your exact vehicle, not a guess.

Condition is the second lever. Pet hair, stains, and a neglected interior add time, and time is the price. Photos help. A detailer who sees the back seat before the visit quotes you once, not twice.

Travel is the third lever, and it’s small but real. Kim adds $20 a visit for Kirkland, Redmond, and Issaquah. That’s an Eastside charge on a Seattle list, and it tells you the pattern: a mobile crew may add a fee at the edge of its area. Ask about it up front if you’re outside the crew’s home ZIPs.
