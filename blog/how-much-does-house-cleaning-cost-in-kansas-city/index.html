How much does house cleaning cost in Kansas City, Kansas?

The short answer. A standard house cleaning in Kansas City, Kansas runs $105 to $220 a visit on MaidProfit’s Kansas City, KS row, and $115 to $240 on its Overland Park row. Hourly, that’s $23 to $38 per cleaner in Kansas City, KS and $25 to $42 in Overland Park. Care.com puts Kansas City, KS at $20.15 an hour starting rate, and Overland Park at about $20.62 an hour on average.

This is where people overpay. Two quotes for the same house can sit $100 apart, and both can be honest. One is a team with its own supplies. The other is one cleaner, by the hour, using your vacuum. Ask what is in the visit before you compare the number.

Why Overland Park costs more than the rest of Kansas

Van Squads coverage for this category sits in Overland Park, and so do the highest prices in the state. All five listings in this metro cover ZIP 66210, with the featured business also covering 66212. MaidProfit says it plainly: Overland Park has the highest prices in Kansas, and the Johnson County suburbs carry the highest rates in the state. Its Kansas City, KS row is $105 to $220 for a standard clean. Its Overland Park row is $115 to $240. Same metro, same kind of house, and the Overland Park visit runs about $10 to $20 higher at each end.

MaidProfit puts it down to higher incomes in Overland Park and Olathe, and notes Johnson County has some of the highest household incomes in the state. Care.com shows the same split: Kansas City, KS starts at $20.15 an hour, while Leawood starts at $26.00 and Prairie Village at $26.57. A quote from a cheaper suburb isn’t a quote for a house in Nall Hills or Oak Park.

Does Kansas charge sales tax on house cleaning?

Yes, and this catches people out. MaidProfit’s Kansas guide says cleaning services are taxable in Kansas: 6.5% state sales tax, plus local taxes that vary by city and county. On a $200 clean, that’s at least $13 on top, before the local piece. Ask whether the quote includes tax. A $180 quote with tax added at the end is $192 or more by the time you pay. A $195 quote that already includes it is cheaper.

Basements add real square footage here. MaidProfit’s Kansas notes say Kansas homes often have basements and large open floor plans, and to factor that in when pricing. A finished basement is not a free room. Say whether it’s in or out before you book. Same for inside the oven and fridge: Audra Rose charges $25 per appliance, and its deep clean starts at $180 against $100 for a maintenance clean. Deep work is a different job, not a longer version of the same visit.

How to pay less and still get a real clean

  • Book the second visit first. Recurring weekly runs $85 to $190 statewide, against $100 to $225 for a one-off standard clean. The first visit is the expensive one.
  • Clear the counters and the floors. A cleaner who spends twenty minutes moving things isn’t cleaning for twenty minutes. A house that hasn’t been cleaned in months costs more — MaidProfit says so directly.
  • Name the extras on the phone. Inside the oven, inside the fridge, the basement. Named up front, they sit in the quote. Found on the day, they sit on top of it.
  • Compare flat lists, not hourly rates alone. A $20-an-hour cleaner who takes five hours costs $100. A $200 flat visit may take three. Ask how long the visit takes.
