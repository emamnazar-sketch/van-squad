How much does pressure washing cost in Sioux Falls?

The short answer. In Sioux Falls, pressure washing a house runs about $500 on average on HomeBlue's Sioux Falls guide — $400–$700 for a 1,500 to 2,000 sq ft house. A driveway averages about $200 there, $150–$260 for a standard 40 x 20 ft driveway. Masters' Guild, a second Sioux Falls guide, puts a one-story house wash lower, at $230–$360 — it prices by story, not house size.

What changes the price in Sioux Falls?

Winter is the big bill here. Sioux Falls gets 44.5 inches of snow in a normal year (Masters' Guild), and what melts off leaves salt and grime behind. Window Genie of Sioux Falls warns that salt deposits build up on concrete and eat away at the material; Angi's Sioux Falls guide says local washing exists to remove that salt and grime from siding, decks, and driveways. A driveway washed every spring is a short job. One that sat through three winters isn't.

Summer adds its own film. Angi's Sioux Falls guide lists prairie dust, pollen, and mildew from humid summers and the Big Sioux River, plus hard water stains on shaded, north-facing walls. That green film on the north side of a house in McKennan Park or the Cathedral district is soft wash work — solution and dwell time — not a harder blast.

Size and stories set the base. HomeBlue climbs by house size, up to $900–$1,600 at 4,000–5,000 sq ft. Height, tight access, and landscaping in the way all push a quote up.

Timing is a real limit, not a sales line. Angi puts routine washing in stable weather, typically May to September, and warns against freezing months, when water can cause damage. Book spring early.

Should your house be pressure washed or soft washed?

Concrete takes high pressure. Siding doesn't. Window Genie of Sioux Falls washes houses with a low-pressure soft wash, chemicals doing the work instead of force — the right ask for vinyl, stucco, and painted wood.

Hot water is the upsell to price carefully. HomeBlue notes power washing — heated water — costs 10–15% more than its cold-water Sioux Falls prices. It earns that on grease and oil. On salt film and pollen, it usually doesn't.

How to pay less and still get it clean

  • Bundle surfaces in one visit. House, driveway, and front walk in one stop — the crew is already set up. A 200 sq ft deck runs $70–$100 as its own job (HomeBlue); as an add-on it should cost less.
  • Wash in spring, once. One wash after snowmelt clears the whole winter. A fall wash gets salted over again by January.
  • Get the measuring stick in writing. By house size, by story, or per square foot — and per square foot of what? The two Sioux Falls guides differ mostly on this, not on the work.
  • Send photos of the worst spots. Salt lines, oil stains, the green north wall. HomeBlue lists heavy dirt, mold, and mildew as Sioux Falls price-raisers — a photo quote beats a surprise on the day.
