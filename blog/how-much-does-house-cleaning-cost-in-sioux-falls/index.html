How much does house cleaning cost in Sioux Falls?

The short answer. A standard house cleaning in Sioux Falls runs $105–$225 a visit from a company, or $23–$38 an hour per cleaner (MaidProfit, Sioux Falls row). Statewide, a standard clean averages $150. Hiring a person on your own costs less — Sioux Falls maids post an average of $16 an hour on Care.com — but that's one person, not a crew. If they call in sick, nobody comes.

Sioux Falls is the priciest cleaning market in South Dakota — biggest city, strongest demand (MaidProfit). The statewide standard average of $150 still sits 21% under the $190 national average on the same page. So a $225 Sioux Falls quote isn't a coastal price. It's the top of a low-cost state.

This is where people overpay: the first visit. A first clean on a home that hasn't been touched in months is priced like a deep clean, $210–$415 statewide. If a company quotes you deep-clean money and calls it a standard first visit, ask what the second visit costs. That number tells you the real rate.

What changes the price in Sioux Falls?

Winter, mostly. South Dakota winters mean snow, mud and road salt tracked across your floors for months (MaidProfit). Entryways and mudrooms in ZIPs 57103 through 57110 take the beating, and a spring booking after a full winter of grit is a longer job than the same rooms in July.

Frequency. Recurring visits cost less statewide: weekly $80–$180, biweekly $85–$195, monthly $100–$215 (MaidProfit). The cleaner knows your home, and the buildup never gets ahead of them.

Size and bathrooms. The per-square-foot figures on the Sioux Falls cost page run $0.09–$0.15, and the size table climbs from $152–$247 under 1,000 sq ft to $174–$323 at 2,001–4,000 sq ft. Bathrooms carry the scrubbing time, so a 3-bed, 3-bath prices above a 3-bed, 2-bath at the same square footage.

Tax is on top. Cleaning services are taxable in South Dakota — 4.5% state tax plus local tax, 6.5% total in Sioux Falls (MaidProfit). A $150 quote can land at about $160 on the invoice. Ask whether your quote already includes it.

Company crew or a person you hire yourself?

At $16 an hour on Care.com, a listed individual costs well under a company's $23–$38 per cleaner. The trade-off is simple. If that person is sick or on vacation, nobody comes. A company sends a replacement, brings supplies, and carries the insurance. Plenty of households hire the individual and accept the gaps. Just price the gaps honestly.

How to pay less and still get a good clean

  • Go recurring after the first visit. Weekly and biweekly rates run well under one-time prices statewide. Pay the higher first-visit price once, then let the cheaper schedule hold it.
  • Name the extras before the visit. Inside the oven, inside the fridge, interior windows — say it up front. Add-ons named mid-clean get priced mid-clean.
  • Don't stop at the city line. Van Squads lists house cleaning across Sioux Falls ZIPs 57103–57110 and out to Brandon (57005), Harrisburg (57032) and Tea (57064). In a newer subdivision out that way, you still have local options — no need for a cleaner driving in from across town.
  • Clear the entryway in winter. Boots and mats piled by the door eat paid minutes. A clear mudroom is a cheaper clean.
