How much does house cleaning cost in Lincoln?

The short answer. A standard house cleaning in Lincoln runs $100 to $220 a visit on MaidProfit’s Lincoln row, at $22 to $38 an hour per cleaner from a company. An individual cleaner costs less per hour — Care.com puts Lincoln’s starting rate at $20.00 an hour, and Housekeeper.com puts the Lincoln median at $20 an hour. Deep and move-out cleans cost more, and the first visit is usually the priciest one.

That gap is not a mistake. The $20 figures are individual cleaners posting their own rates. The $22 to $38 range is what companies charge per cleaner, and it carries insurance, supplies, and a second cleaner when needed.

This is where people overpay in Lincoln: comparing an individual’s hourly rate against a company’s flat visit price, without counting how many hours — and cleaners — each one puts in your home.

How much does home size change the price in Lincoln?

Size sets the visit price more than anything else. TheCleanestRoom’s Nebraska table puts a standard 3-bedroom clean at $125 to $160, with a state average near $150 a visit.

Bathrooms matter almost as much. Each extra bathroom adds time on every visit, not just the first one.

In Lincoln that plays out street by street. A smaller home near East Campus in Woods Park (68510) is a shorter job than a larger family home in South Lincoln (68516) or Piedmont and Eastridge (68506). Same city, same rate card, very different visit price.

Why does the first clean cost more in Lincoln?

The first visit is almost always a deep clean, even if nobody calls it that. Kitchens and bathrooms carry the buildup, and it has to go before a standard visit can hold.

TheCleanestRoom shows the step for a 3-bedroom home: $125 to $160 standard, $180 to $235 deep. Nebraska Elite Cleaning, which serves the Lincoln metro, says smaller, simpler jobs may begin around $200, with many detailed cleanings between about $250 and $1,000 or more.

After that, a regular schedule brings the price down. A home cleaned every two weeks stays near the low end of the standard range.

When does move-out cleaning spike in Lincoln?

Lincoln has a rental rhythm that bigger, non-college cities do not. MaidProfit’s Nebraska page notes it directly: the University of Nebraska creates seasonal move-out demand in Lincoln.

That means late spring and late summer get busy. Leases turn over, and the good slots go first. TheCleanestRoom puts a Nebraska 3-bedroom move-out at $195 to $265, and Care.com puts Lincoln move-out cleaning at a $20.64 an hour starting rate.

If your move is tied to a semester end, book early. A move-out clean is also a different job — inside cabinets, the oven and fridge, and closets are part of it, the things a landlord checks at the walk-through.

What should you ask before you book in Lincoln?

Ask whether the price is per hour or per visit, and how many cleaners are coming. A $22 an hour quote with two cleaners is a different bill from $38 an hour with one.

Ask what the first visit includes, and who brings the supplies. Housekeeper.com notes Lincoln cleaners who bring their own supplies charge toward the high end of its $15 to $30 range — fair, as long as you know it before you compare quotes.
