How Much Does House Cleaning Cost in Oklahoma City?

The short answer. In Oklahoma City, an independent maid averages about $15 an hour (Care.com, October 2026). A recurring clean for a 1,001–2,000 sq ft home runs $95 a visit weekly, $110 biweekly, or $140 monthly on a published local price list (Clean Pros). A one-time clean for a standard 1,500 sq ft home runs $150 to $300, and a deep cleaning runs $200 to $400.

Oklahoma City sits well below the national average: HomeAdvisor put the average Oklahoma City cleaning at about $127 a visit, against a national average near $175. Frequency is the biggest lever after size — the same 2,000 sq ft home costs $95 on a weekly plan and $140 as a monthly visit on the local list above.

What changes the price in Oklahoma City?

  • Home size, priced by the square foot. Most Oklahoma City services quote by square footage and frequency, not by the hour. Bathrooms add time on top of the square-foot baseline, so a 3-bed, 3-bath in Edmond will quote higher than a 3-bed, 2-bath of the same size in south Oklahoma City.
  • First clean vs. recurring. Expect the first visit to cost more — on the Clean Pros list, a one-time deep clean is priced at twice the weekly rate for the same home. Once a home is on a weekly or biweekly rhythm, each visit starts from a maintained level and the per-visit price drops.
  • The type of clean. A standard visit covers kitchens, bathrooms, floors, and dusting. Deep cleaning adds baseboards, grout, inside appliances, and window tracks. Move-in and move-out cleans are priced by bedroom count and run highest in late spring through early fall, when lease turnover peaks in Oklahoma City — book two to three weeks ahead in August, especially for rentals in Norman.
  • Where you live in the metro. Van Squads lists house cleaners covering Oklahoma City itself (73120), Edmond (73012), and Norman (73072). Homes inside a cleaner’s regular route rarely see a travel charge; homes well outside it may see a higher minimum.

How to pay less without getting a worse clean

  • Book recurring. Weekly is cheapest per visit, biweekly is the sweet spot for most Oklahoma City homes — frequent enough that no visit starts from scratch.
  • Bundle the extras into one visit. Fridge, oven, and interior windows added to a scheduled clean cost less than booking them separately.
  • Get a flat quote by square foot, in writing. Ask for the whole-job price for your home’s size and frequency before the first visit, so the hourly clock is not your problem.

How much does a house cleaner charge per hour in Oklahoma City?

How much is a standard house cleaning in Oklahoma City?

For a 1,001–2,000 sq ft home, $95 a visit weekly, $110 biweekly, or $140 monthly on a published local price list (Clean Pros). A one-time clean for a 1,500 sq ft home runs $150–$300 (CottageCare, Downtown Oklahoma City).

How much does deep cleaning cost in Oklahoma City?

$200 to $400 for a typical home (CottageCare). On the Clean Pros list, a one-time deep clean is priced at twice the weekly rate for the same square footage.

What is included in a standard house cleaning?

Kitchen countertops, stovetop, and appliance exteriors, bathroom scrubbing, floor vacuuming and mopping, and dusting throughout. Inside ovens and fridges, interior windows, and laundry are usually add-ons — confirm before the visit.
