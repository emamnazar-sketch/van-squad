Read the spread the right way. A $175 starting package and a $370 full detail are not the same job with a different markup. The cheaper one is about 1.5 hours with no shampooing. The $370 one runs 5–6 hours with a clay bar, a sealant, and a deep interior shampoo, per Enchanted’s own list. This is where people overpay — comparing two numbers without comparing the hours and the work inside them.

Why does Santa Fe dust and sun change the job?

Santa Fe sits high and dry, and your car shows it. Fine dust works into vents, cupholders, and carpet, and it comes back fast on the dirt and gravel drives on the south side, in ZIP 87507. A vacuum-only interior looks dusty again in a week if the vents and crevices were skipped. Ask whether the interior price covers vent and crevice work, not just seats and mats.

Sun is the other half. At this elevation the sun fades untreated leather and cracks dashboards faster than it does at sea level — Platinum’s Albuquerque interior package includes dash UV protection for exactly that high-desert reason, and the same logic holds in Santa Fe. If your car lives outside in 87505, UV protection on the dash and trim is not an upsell. It is the part of the job that keeps the next detail cheaper.

Water matters too. A rinse that dries in the Santa Fe sun can bake mineral spots into paint that was just cleaned — Enchanted carries deionized water on its rig for that reason. Ask a mobile detailer what water he brings, or whether he connects to your hose.

What about water, power, and a place to work?

Not every mobile detailer brings everything. Platinum brings its own water and power in Albuquerque. Others connect to your hose and an outlet. In Santa Fe that question is real: a home in 87501 with street parking and no outside outlet is a different job from a driveway in 87505 with a spigot by the garage.

Ask three things before you book. Do you bring water and power? How much flat, shaded space do you need? And what happens if the wind picks up — dust blowing onto a wet car is a Santa Fe problem a shop never has. A detailer who answers those plainly has worked here before.
