How much does mobile car detailing cost in Grand Forks?

The short answer. Grand Forks has no published mobile-only price list, so use two anchors. The local shop benchmark is Rydell Detail Center: a Full Interior or Full Exterior detail starts at $209, its Total Detail at $259, and its Showroom Shine at $359. Nationally, Thumbtack puts a mobile detail at $144 to $254 on average in 2026, with basic work from about $95. A mobile quote between those two anchors is in the normal band. One above the shop’s $359 top package needs a written reason.

Read the two blocks differently. Rydell’s list is a shop list — you drop the car off on South Washington Street. This is where people overpay in Grand Forks. They pay a Total Detail price for what is really a wash, a vacuum, and a wipe-down. Before you book, ask which list the package matches: interior only, exterior only, or both — and get the add-ons priced one by one, the way Rydell prints them.

Why does winter set the detailing calendar in Grand Forks?

Salt. Rydell’s own winter guide for Grand Forks is blunt: the road salt that keeps the roads clear speeds up corrosion, the undercarriage takes the worst of it because it gets splashed with brine all winter, and brakes and other parts under the car sit right in that spray. Salt drying on the paint damages the finish too.

That is why the $50 undercarriage wash on Rydell’s add-on list is the most local line in the table. A shiny hood with a salty underside is only half a detail in this town. If a mobile quote for a Grand Forks winter car says nothing about the underside, wheel wells, or rocker panels, ask. The average high here stays below freezing from December through February on Rydell’s guide, so a driveway detail in deep winter may need a heated garage or a warmer day — ask where the work happens before you book a January date.

Spring is the other booking crunch. Four months of brine, slush packed in the wheel wells, and salt tracked onto the mats all come due at once. Book the spring detail when the slush starts, not after it ends.

What does the University of North Dakota do to prices and timing?

UND is the second calendar in town. Rydell runs a students page that offers to schedule detailing around classes, with drop-off and pickup — a shop saying out loud that student cars are a real slice of its work.

The practical points are timing and condition. A car that sat outside near campus all winter comes out of spring with salt film outside and a semester of grit inside. That is deep-clean work, not a basic package, and it prices that way. May fills up too: students leaving town want cars cleaned before the drive home, the same weeks everyone else wants the winter washed off. Book early in May, or wait for June.

How much do trucks and SUVs add?

Size is the cleanest price step on every list. Jobber’s national full-detail ranges put a sedan at $150 to $250, an SUV at $200 to $350, and a truck at $200 to $500. Rydell’s Grand Forks packages are also priced by vehicle size, with its published range running to $459 at the top.

A pickup that works — farm and construction trucks are normal traffic here — sits at the top of its band when the bed and mats carry a season of grit. Send photos of the bed and the driver’s footwell before you book. A quote given sight unseen on a work truck is a guess, and guesses get revised at your door.

What does coverage in ZIPs 58201, 58202, 58203, and 56721 actually cover?

Our directory’s Grand Forks mobile detailing coverage sits in four ZIPs. ZIPs 58201, 58202, and 58203 cover Grand Forks itself — downtown and the Near Southside, the University District around UND, Riverside and Lincoln Park along the river, the Columbia Mall area, and the South End. ZIP 56721 is East Grand Forks, across the Red River in Minnesota, covered by one listing in this cell.

Say which side of the river you are on in the first message. A detailer based on the North Dakota side may price the East Grand Forks trip differently, and you want that in the quote, not at the door.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a full detail in Grand Forks?

At Rydell Detail Center, the Grand Forks shop benchmark, a Total Detail starts at $259 and the top Showroom Shine package starts at $359, with its published range running $209 to $459 by vehicle size. Nationally, a full mobile detail runs $150 to $250 for a sedan and $200 to $350 for an SUV on Jobber’s 2026 ranges.

Is mobile detailing cheaper than a shop detail in Grand Forks?

On the national numbers, often yes. Thumbtack’s 2026 mobile average is $144 to $254, below Rydell’s $209 starting prices in Grand Forks. What you give up is the shop itself — a lift, a heated bay in January, and a ride while you wait. What you gain is your own driveway and your own schedule.

What does an undercarriage wash cost in Grand Forks, and do I need one?

Rydell lists it at $50 as an add-on. After a Grand Forks winter, yes — its winter guide says the undercarriage takes constant brine spray and rusts fastest there, with brakes and other underside parts exposed. At minimum, get the underside flushed once in spring.

Why does a truck cost more to detail than a sedan?

More surface, more cabin, and usually more grit. Jobber’s national ranges put a truck at $200 to $500 against $150 to $250 for a sedan, and Rydell also prices its Grand Forks packages by vehicle size. A work truck with a dirty bed sits at the top of the band.

When should I book a detail in Grand Forks?

Twice a year covers most cars here: once in fall before salt season, with wax or sealant on the paint, and once in spring to get four months of brine off the underside and out of the mats. May is the crowded month — UND students and everyone else book the same weeks.
