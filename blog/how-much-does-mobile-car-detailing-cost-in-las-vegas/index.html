How much does mobile car detailing cost in Las Vegas?

The short answer. A full mobile detail in Las Vegas runs $250 to $700 on AOA Detailing’s Las Vegas guide, by vehicle size and package. Lighter lists start lower — a full detail from $100 for a sedan at 3 In One LLC, and a Refresh full detail from $200 for a sedan at Bartlett Mobile Detail. Size and condition move the quote more than anything else, so send photos before you book.

That spread looks odd until you read what is inside each package. A $100 full detail and a $425 Revive full detail are not the same job. The Revive level adds clay decontamination outside and steam plus hot extraction inside, per Bartlett’s list. The cheap end is a thorough clean. The high end is a reset for a car that has been let go.

This is where people overpay in Las Vegas: buying the deep package for a car that only needed the refresh, or buying the cheap wash for a car with baked-on water spots and then paying twice.

Why size and condition set the price here

Every Las Vegas list above prices by size first. At 3 In One, a full detail steps from $100 for a sedan to $150 for a large SUV, truck or van. At FSD, the same step is $225 to $325. Bigger vehicle, more panels, more seats, more time.

Condition is the second lever. Pet hair, stains, odors and a neglected interior all push a starting price up — 3 In One and FSD both say so on their lists, and both confirm the final price before work starts. Ask for that confirmation in writing. A starting price is not a quote.

Desert sun and hard water do the damage in Las Vegas

Las Vegas cars do not get dirty the way cars in wet places do. They bake.

AOA’s Las Vegas guide names desert sun, UV exposure and the valley’s hard water as price factors up front. Bartlett makes the same point from the work side: water spot removal is built into its 2-step correction work because Nevada hard water leaves marks a normal wash will not lift.

That matters in Silverado Ranch (89123) — the ZIP every business in the featured section below covers — and across the south valley in Paradise (89119) and Southwest Las Vegas (89113, 89141). A car parked outside all day needs protection on the paint, not just a wash. A spray wax or sealant on a regular detail holds up better here than a one-off deep clean once a year.

Book early in the day in summer if you can. Shaded, cooler panels wash cleaner, and a good mobile detailer will tell you that before you pick a time.

Ceramic coating — worth it, or not?

AOA puts ceramic coating in Las Vegas at $1,000 to $1,650, with paint correction at $550 to $950 before it. That is real money. It makes sense on a car you plan to keep, parked outside in this sun. It makes little sense on a car you plan to sell next spring.

Ask what prep is included. Coating over uncorrected paint locks in the water spots and swirls you already have. The correction is not an upsell in that case. It is the job.

What do add-ons cost in Las Vegas?

Does hard water change the price in Las Vegas?

It can. AOA names the valley’s hard water as a pricing factor, and Bartlett includes water spot removal in its 2-step correction work because Nevada hard water marks paint. Light spots wash off. Baked-on spots need correction work, priced at $550 to $950 on AOA’s guide.
