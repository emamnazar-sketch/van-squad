How much does house cleaning cost in Omaha?

The short answer. In Omaha, a standard clean runs $110 to $235 a visit (MaidProfit, Omaha figures). Every-other-week visits run $145 to $300 or more (Pink Shoe Cleaning Crew, an Omaha company). A cleaner starts at $20.54 an hour on Care.com as of October 2026. Deep cleans cost more. So do first visits.

The hourly gap trips people up. Care.com's $20.54 is a starting rate for cleaners listed there, often one person you hire direct. Company crews in Omaha run $24 to $40 an hour per cleaner (MaidProfit). A direct hire may not include insurance, bonding, or a backup cleaner on a sick day (Pink Shoe). Compare the whole deal, not just the hourly number.

This is where people overpay: paying a deep-clean price for a home that only needs a standard visit, or booking one-time cleans again and again. Weekly service usually brings the per-visit rate down by roughly 10% against every-other-week (Pink Shoe). One Omaha company takes 20% off weekly visits from the second clean onward (Adalyn's).

What changes the price in Omaha?

Home size and bathrooms. Bathrooms take the most detail work per square foot, so they move the quote fast (Pink Shoe). Adalyn's builds its Omaha flat rate from bedrooms first — a studio starts at $119 and each bedroom adds $10 — with bathrooms priced on top.

The basement. Nebraska homes often have basements, and a finished basement adds real cleaning area (MaidProfit). A ranch in Northwest Omaha (68116, 68134) with a finished lower level is a bigger job than the square footage upstairs suggests. Say whether the basement is in or out before you book.

Which side of the metro you are on. Care.com puts Omaha starting pay at $20.54 an hour, but Elkhorn starts at $22.44 and Papillion at $23.62, while Bellevue starts lower, at $19.16. A quote in Elkhorn or Papillion that sits above a central Omaha quote is following the local rates, not padding.

First clean vs. recurring. A first visit can take about three times as long as a normal one while the home is brought up to a maintenance level (Pink Shoe). After that, a two-person crew usually finishes a top-to-bottom clean in one to three hours.

Pets and buildup. Pet hair adds vacuuming time, and a home that has gone months without a clean takes longer than one on a schedule (Pink Shoe; MaidProfit). Name both up front and the quote holds.

How to pay less without getting a worse clean

  • Pick a schedule and keep it. Weekly visits cost about 10% less per visit than every-other-week ones in Omaha (Pink Shoe). Skipping months and booking catch-up cleans costs more in the end.
  • Get a flat price in writing. Bedrooms, bathrooms and square footage are enough for a flat quote. An open hourly quote on a big West Omaha home can drift.
  • Pick up clutter before the crew arrives. Cleaners work around piles instead of moving them, and that slows the visit (Pink Shoe). Your money should buy cleaning time, not tidying time.
  • Near Offutt? Plan move-out cleans early. The base by Bellevue keeps relocation cleaning in steady demand around the south metro (MaidProfit). Book the empty-home clean before your move date is locked, not after.
