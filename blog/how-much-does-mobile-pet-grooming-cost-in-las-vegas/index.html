How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Las Vegas?

The short answer. A mobile dog groom in Las Vegas starts at $85 for a small dog, $95 to $125 for a medium dog, $140 to $185 for a large dog, and $200 or more for an extra-large dog on Fluffy Pet Wash’s Las Vegas list. Haircuts add $35 to $50 on that list. Other Las Vegas lists start lower — one starts at $65 — so compare what is included, not just the first number.

That spread is mostly about what is bundled. Fluffy’s price is all-inclusive — wash, blowout, nails, ears, deshedding — with the haircut as the only add-on. A $65 starting price on another list can climb once a cut, matting work, or a big dog enters the picture.

This is where people overpay in Las Vegas: comparing a bare starting price against an all-in price and booking the bare one, then paying add-ons at the van.

Why size and coat set the price here

Every Las Vegas list above prices by size first. At Fluffy, the step from small to extra-large is $85 to $200 or more. At Bark Of The Town, it is $89 to $130 or more. Bigger dog, more coat, more time in the van.

Coat is the second lever. Doodles and poodles carry a specialized breed fee of up to $50 at Fluffy when a haircut or matting is involved — and that fee already includes the haircut, so the two do not stack. Matting is billed by time at Well Groomed Mobile, $20 per 15 minutes. Ten minutes of brushing at home between visits is the cheapest grooming you will ever buy.

Desert heat changes the groom, not just the schedule

Las Vegas grooming has a heat problem that cooler cities do not.

Kari’s Groom Room, a Las Vegas groomer, puts it plainly: shaving a double-coated dog short does not keep it cooler. The coat insulates and shields the skin from direct sun. Shaving it off raises the sunburn risk. The better summer job is undercoat removal — loose undercoat out, protective outer coat left in place — so air moves through the coat.

That matters in Henderson (89052) — the ZIP every business in the featured section below covers — and across Green Valley (89014, 89074), Summerlin (89135), Spring Valley (89147), and Silverado Ranch (89123). A husky or shepherd in this valley needs deshedding on a schedule, not a summer shave.

Book the early slot in summer if your dog allows it. Fluffy runs 7 AM to 10 PM across the valley, and a morning groom keeps your dog out of a hot driveway handoff at midday.

Cats cost more, and there is a reason

Mobile cat grooming starts at $120 at Fluffy, with a lion cut about $35 extra. Cats take one calm handler, a quiet van, and no dogs nearby. You are paying for that setup, not just the bath.

Care.com’s Las Vegas average of $16.05 an hour is for individual pet groomers, not van services. It is useful as a floor for in-home help, not as a quote for a mobile van visit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile pet grooming cost in Las Vegas?

On Fluffy Pet Wash’s Las Vegas list, an all-inclusive groom starts at $85 for a small dog, $95 to $125 for a medium dog, $140 to $185 for a large dog, and $200 or more for an extra-large dog. Haircuts add $35 for small and medium dogs or $50 for large dogs. Thumbtack’s national average for dog grooming is $60 to $80, labeled national.

How much does a large doodle cost to groom in Las Vegas?

Fluffy Pet Wash gives a worked example: a large doodle with a haircut is $140 plus a $50 specialized breed fee, $190 total. That breed fee already includes the haircut, so the separate haircut add-on is not charged on top.

How much does mobile cat grooming cost in Las Vegas?

Mobile cat grooming starts at $120 at Fluffy Pet Wash, and the price can rise with coat condition and temperament. A lion cut is usually about $35 extra.

Does mobile grooming cost more than a salon in Las Vegas?

Usually, yes. HomeGuide puts a mobile full service for an average-size dog at about $75 in Las Vegas, against salon full-service prices starting around $40 for a small dog on the same page. You pay the difference for the van at your door and one-on-one handling.

Should I shave my double-coated dog for the Las Vegas summer?

Las Vegas groomers generally say no. Kari’s Groom Room explains that a double coat insulates and protects the skin from sun, and shaving raises the sunburn risk. Undercoat removal is the recommended summer job instead — it is included in Fluffy’s all-inclusive groom.
