How much does house cleaning cost in Phoenix?

The short answer. Most Phoenix homeowners pay $120 to $250 a visit for a standard recurring clean and $250 to $500 for a one-time deep clean, on Queen of Maids’ Phoenix guide. Angi’s Phoenix page puts a professional clean at $172 on average, ranging $120 to $231. MaidProfit’s Arizona row is close: a standard clean averages $175, ranging $120 to $260. By the hour, Care.com puts Phoenix house cleaning at a $22.07 starting rate, and Tempe at about $20.75 an hour.

This is where people overpay. A $135 flat-rate visit and a $390 one-time visit can both be honest Phoenix quotes. The low end is a small home on a flat-rate list, or a recurring visit in a tidy home. The high end is a one-time clean on a bigger home. Ask what the visit covers, and whether the first visit is priced as a deep clean, before you compare two numbers.

One spread is worth naming. Desert First’s $390 one-time price for a 3-bed, 2-bath home sits well above Angi’s $164 to $205 for the same size. Both are shown under their own names. Neither is your quote — size, condition, and schedule decide that.

Dust is the standing job in Phoenix

Desert dust doesn’t take a week off. Queen of Maids’ Phoenix guide names heavy dust as a reason a home prices toward the top of its range, and it calls out monsoon and dust-storm season in particular. Greater Phoenix Cleaning puts dates on it: monsoon runs June 15 to September 30, and those weeks leave dust on sills, ledges, and baseboards.

That changes what a standard visit has to do here. In Tempe (85281), which all 9 Van Squads house cleaning listings in this metro cover, and across central Phoenix, the recurring work is floors, sills, and surfaces that collect a fine layer fast — not just kitchens and bathrooms. If your home sits near an open desert lot, say so when you book. It’s real cleaning time.

Hard water adds time in Phoenix bathrooms

Phoenix water is hard. Greater Phoenix Cleaning, citing the City of Phoenix 2025 Water Quality Report, puts total hardness at 10 to 17.6 grains per gallon, and notes that mineral film comes back on shower glass, faucets, and fixtures between visits.

You see it as white spots that don’t wipe off with a quick spray. Queen of Maids lists scale and grout work in bathrooms as part of a deep clean, not a standard visit. So if your shower glass and fixtures have built up film, expect the first visit to be priced as a deep clean — $250 to $500 on its Phoenix guide, or $290 to $350 for a 3-bed, 2-bath home on Angi’s page — and the cheaper recurring price to start after that reset.

How to pay less and still get a clean house

  • Go recurring. Angi’s Phoenix page shows weekly at $160 to $250 a visit against monthly at $180 to $320, and most Phoenix companies discount recurring work by 10% to 20% on that page. Maid Complete’s Phoenix list takes 15% off weekly, 10% off biweekly, and 5% off monthly.
  • Do the deep clean once, on purpose. Paying $250 to $500 once so later visits stay at the standard rate beats paying deep-clean time in small pieces every month.
  • Clear counters and floors before the visit. Hourly and flat-rate quotes both assume the crew can start cleaning when they walk in.
  • Skip add-ons you won’t notice. Angi prices window cleaning at $4 to $7 a window and inside appliances at $12 to $50 in Phoenix. Add them to one visit a quarter, not every visit.
